# Current contribution position: AI method, grounded in established physics

This is a claim boundary, not a completed novelty certification.

The inherited BGFM story centered on force/energy losses added to an existing
FlowMol3 backbone and arguments linking them to Boltzmann behavior. New losses
can be substantive AI contributions. Here, however, incorrect density/head
semantics and insufficient independent sampling evidence weakened the original
claims; calling it simply "no AI novelty" would also overstate the audit.

The current candidate is a learned invertible molecular transport acting on a
frozen implicit generator. Element-group internal and centroid maps use invariant
neural contexts and nonlinear convex point maps. Its centering-corrected intrinsic
volume is exact, using3-by-3 factorizations. Training uses energy and the exact
entropy change rather than an estimated source score or absolute likelihood.
This is concrete implemented algorithm/architecture work, with an affine
same-context ablation. It is still a candidate contribution: we must establish
its distinction from prior work, useful cross-condition effects and fair costs.

We do not claim new physics. Energy–entropy competition, Gibbs targets,
change of variables and nonequilibrium work identities are established. Inversion
averaging repairs consistency for this chosen source/potential, and COM volume
accounting makes the method mathematically coherent. Neither a correctness repair
nor AFM/Jarzynski motivation alone supplies the paper's novelty.

Relevant established foundations include
[Boltzmann Generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01729),
[Equivariant Flows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.02425), and
[EACF](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10364v6).
"Invertible network plus energy training" and "equivariant molecular flow" are
not new claims. The intended distinction is the particular constrained molecular
transport and a demonstrated benefit of exact marginal entropy refinement of an
implicit base. General KL cancellation and determinant identities are standard
mathematics; do not advertise them as newly discovered laws or theorems.

The optional fixed-index split remains a labelled diagnostic. It breaks
same-element permutation equivariance and retains subgroup centroids. It cannot
support a symmetry-preserving homogeneous extension claim. The broad main
campaign still uses the original whole-element architecture.

Current evidence is encouraging but mixed. Condition0 favors the nonlinear model
over both affine and typed controls in two training streams; condition1 favors
it over affine but does not show a typed-control gain. Condition0's independent
GFN2 panel has192 converged assessments and32 failures out of224 total, so the
lower successful-sample strain is not a full validity certificate. No calibrated
population coverage or strong external-baseline superiority is established.

The intended paper is therefore an AI sampling/refinement method for a declared
physical target, not a new physical mechanism paper. Its ICLR case must rest on
the actual architecture/algorithm, defensible theory, meaningful replicated
results and comparisons. More implementation alone cannot establish that case.
