\section{Chemical geometry and illustrative structures}\label{app:chemical-geometry}
We supplement graph inference and the GFN2 force criterion with geometric checks
from PoseBusters 0.4.4 \citep{buttenschoen2024posebusters}. The checks use distance
bounds for heavy-atom bond lengths and angles, internal steric distances, planar
aromatic rings and double bonds, and nonplanarity of saturated six-membered
rings. We use the package's molecule-configuration tolerances: 25\% for bond and
angle bounds, 30\% for internal distances, 0.25\,\AA\ for planar groups, and
0.05\,\AA\ for nonplanar rings. A structure must also have zero assigned radical
electrons. The check is a geometric subset of PoseBusters; it omits conformer
ensemble energy ratios and reference-identity comparisons.

Every generated coordinate remains fixed. We evaluate all 5,120 attempts per
setting, using the same inferred graph as the original benchmark. This includes
outputs with failed graph inference in the denominator. Table~\ref{tab:chemical-geometry}
reports the geometric subset alone and its conjunction with the original
GFN2 force threshold of 5 eV/\AA.

For FM, correction reduces contact-disconnected outputs from 3,092 to 2,702,
heavy-atom-disconnected outputs from 2,527 to 2,325, and severe overlaps from
126 to nine. Among the 1,273 graph-valid corrected outputs, 674 fail a planarity
check, 340 fail a bond-angle check, 237 fail a bond-length check, and 296 fail
the internal-distance check. These categories overlap. On all 512 prepared
validation references, 470 pass the same closed-shell geometric screen.
The comparison identifies connectivity and detailed geometry as remaining
generation errors even after the force criterion improves.

\paragraph{The molecular illustration.}
Figure~\ref{fig:method} uses an additional validation-composition panel chosen
from reference structures before generation. The selection takes the first four
distinct compositions, in original validation-row order, whose references have
17--28 atoms, CHONF elements, uncharged atoms, five- or six-membered rings, and
pass the geometric checks. Each composition receives 32 raw outputs from the
unchanged main-model fit 1, with correction strength four and the hydrogen
readout disabled. All 128 outputs are preserved and evaluated: 79 pass graph
inference and 15 pass the closed-shell geometric screen.

The displayed $\mathrm{C_9H_9NO_2}$ structure is condition index 2, draw 27
(zero-based), with inferred connectivity \texttt{N=Cc1ccccc1C1OC1O}. It is the
first output in condition/draw order with an aromatic ring, uncharged atoms,
zero assigned radicals, passing geometry checks, and force RMS at most
5 eV/\AA. Its force RMS is 2.886 eV/\AA. An earlier illustration screen that
excluded every three- and four-membered ring had no eligible output; the
displayed example contains a saturated epoxide. This panel supplies an
illustration and is separate from the 64-composition benchmark.

We replay the source random numbers exactly and use the stored output coordinates.
The 3D source and output share camera and scale. The 2D inset depicts the inferred
connectivity, with hydrogens implicit in the usual chemical notation. Bond
orders are rendered from an explicit Kekul\'e representation, and the PyMOL
round-trip check verifies atom identities, bond orders and coordinate precision.
No molecular geometry optimization is used in preparing the illustration.
