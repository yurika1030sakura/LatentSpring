\subsection{Generalization across molecular sizes}\label{sec:wide}
Without further training, we evaluate 64 new compositions, 16 in each of four ranges spanning 17--64 atoms. All are absent from both processed OMol25 corpora and earlier panels. Fixed metadata ranks select eligible references; each fit generates 32 draws per composition.

Pooled graph validity is 24.85\% for Gaussian and 31.76\% for harmonic. Harmonic is higher in every size range (Appendix~\ref{app:wide-size}), with overall gain 6.91 points and size-stratified composition 95\% interval [4.74,9.03]. The two runs gain 11.18 and 2.64 points. Absolute validity decreases with size, but the pooled source advantage persists beyond the original 17--28-atom range.

Adding three runs gives a five-run 3,000-update mean gain of 3.89 points, positive in four runs, with standard deviation 5.27. For the new runs, the mean advantage is $-1.66$ at 1,000 updates, $+1.87$ at 3,000, and $+3.94$ at 6,000; all three favor harmonic at the final budget. Appendix~\ref{app:source-replication} reports all checkpoints.
