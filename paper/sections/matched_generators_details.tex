\section{Independent-backbone comparison}\label{app:matched-generators}
This study tests the source on an independent architecture with no pretraining.
All methods use the official EDM equivariant graph neural network (EGNN)
\citep{edm}, with four message-passing layers, 256 hidden features and
2,381,566 parameters. Within each of two independently initialized repetitions,
all methods start from identical weights and receive the same minibatches.
Atomic types are fixed inputs throughout training and generation.

The common data comprise 20,000 OMol25 training rows spanning 8,323 compositions,
plus 512 internal validation rows spanning 299 other compositions. Both sets use
neutral organic singlets with 8--64 atoms. A composition hash separates training
and internal validation. Reference selection requires the declared geometric and
chemical-graph checks, without the additional scaffold-tree filter used for the
pretrained-model study. None of the 64 evaluation compositions appears in either
subset or either full processed corpus.

Each model receives 30,000 AdamW updates at learning rate $10^{-4}$ and batch
size 32, totaling 960,000 training presentations. Batches contain equal-sized
molecules; selecting atom-count buckets in proportion to their populations gives
uniform training-row marginals. We clip the gradient norm at one. Generation uses
an exponential moving average of network weights at the fixed final update,
with decay $\min(0.999,(1+s)/(10+s))$ at optimizer step $s$.
Internal validation at 5,000, 15,000 and 30,000 updates diagnoses learning and
does not select a checkpoint.

The Gaussian and harmonic FM arms share the linear interpolation, atom matching
and rotation pairing. Both predict velocity with a single EGNN evaluation and use
64 midpoint steps for 128 network evaluations; this comparison has no
self-conditioning. The harmonic source uses the same probability law as the
main model. For its factorized affinities, independently drawing the $N-2$ entries
of a Pr\"ufer sequence with probabilities $u_i/\sum_j u_j$ samples exactly that
tree law. Decoding the sequence gives the tree used to place atoms.

EDM predicts Gaussian coordinate noise under its original variance-preserving
schedule. The position-conditional GAGA variant \citep{qu2026gaga} truncates this
schedule at the published GEOM setting of 650 out of 1,000 steps. Its initial
Gaussian variance is $\alpha_t^2 v+\sigma_t^2$, where $\alpha_t$ and $\sigma_t$
are the schedule's signal and noise coefficients. The training-only estimate
$v=3.0232257866$\,\AA$^2$ averages squared centered coordinates per independent
coordinate. The truncation setting is transferred from GEOM and is not optimized
on OMol25 evaluation outcomes.

All models generate 32 raw structures per evaluation composition at 128 network
evaluations. The complete EDM and GAGA schedules, including the final observation
step, are also reported at 1,001 and 651 evaluations. No physical energy labels,
bond supervision, energy-based selection or geometry relaxation enter this study.
The A100, H200 and A100 MIG allocations are recorded in the scheduler metadata;
matching training presentations and network-call counts does not match wall time.
