\section{Data selection and structural evaluation}\label{app:data-evaluation}
For each source fine-tuning run, we traverse the processed OMol25 training corpus in a fixed shuffled order, exclude evaluation compositions, and retain the first 3,000 eligible structures. Eligibility combines the composition and electronic-state requirements in Appendix~\ref{app:source-study-setup} with the structural and scaffold checks below.

\paragraph{Training scaffold.}
Candidate scaffold edges have pair distance, divided by summed covalent radii, strictly between 0.6501 and 1.1999. Kruskal's algorithm constructs a minimum normalized-distance spanning tree. A reference is accepted when the tree is connected and degrees are at most 1 for H/F/Cl/Br/I, 4 for B/C/N/Si, 3 for O, and 6 for P/S. This screening tree is separate from the random source tree.

\paragraph{Bond inference.}
After contact and overlap checks, we call RDKit 2025.03.2 \texttt{DetermineBonds} at the requested charge, using \texttt{covFactor=1.25} and \texttt{useVdw=True}. We set \texttt{allowChargedFragments=True} and \texttt{embedChiral=False}, then explicitly sanitize the inferred graph. Acceptance requires successful sanitization, the requested sum of formal charges, and a single connected component. For diversity, we remove stereochemical tags and explicit hydrogens and count distinct canonical SMILES. Rejections and validator errors remain in the attempt denominator.

\paragraph{24-composition confirmation panel.}
We freeze the Gaussian, harmonic, and two physically updated harmonic models and designate the force-only update as primary before evaluation. Of 256 candidate compositions outside the earlier sets, 121 pass reference-structure checks. Fixed metadata ranks select eight each with 17--20, 21--24, and 25--28 atoms. All 24 are absent from both processed OMol25 corpora; references are used only for eligibility. Four models, two runs, and 32 draws per composition give 6,144 raw outputs.
