\subsection{Cross-generator weight transfer}\label{app:cross-head}
The transfer protocol is fixed before generating cross-head outputs and evaluating the three new fit pairs. Fits 1--2 already have target-parent results; fits 3--5 provide the primary independent replication. Each target receives the source family's final 20,000-update head with weights, vocabulary, bounds, and strength unchanged. The endpoint conversion in Equation~\ref{eq:endpoint-native-updates} maps its action to the target sampler. Inputs are coordinates, predicted endpoints, atom identities, and normalized progress, without backbone features.

In the three new fits, replacing GAGA's own head with the FM-trained head changes joint yield by $-0.36$ points (composition interval $[-1.66,0.94]$; crossed $[-2.15,1.30]$). Replacing FM's head with the GAGA-trained head changes yield by $+1.50$ points ($[0.33,2.64]$; crossed $[0.00,3.12]$). Both transfer directions improve over the uncorrected target, while comparisons with target-trained heads are smaller and less certain.

All five pairs and both directions are reported. The study adds 10,240 trajectories and 10,240 successful GFN2 calculations, without head updates or new force labels. A metadata-writing restart reuses checksum-verified coordinates and does not duplicate trajectory counts.
