\subsection{Source and physical adaptation}\label{sec:absolute-quality}
We complete the comparison of sources and physical updates by applying the same paired physical
recipe to the Gaussian-source generator. Each source generates its own training
anchors on the same eight training compositions, with the same maximum proposal
budget and training schedule. The Gaussian physical model is evaluated on the
existing 24-composition panel; the other three cells reuse their original outputs.
Appendix~\ref{sec:factorial-details} gives the protocol and preparation costs.

Joint quality yield counts outputs that pass the chemical-graph assay and meet a
force-RMS threshold, divided by every attempted output. Invalid structures and
failed energy calculations remain in the denominator. Table~\ref{tab:absolute-quality}
reports this yield at 5 eV/\AA\ under independent GFN2 evaluation.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Sources and physical updates compared on 1,536 raw attempts per model.
Force medians use each model's own successful, graph-valid outputs; joint yield
uses all attempts and requires graph validity and force RMS at most 5 eV/\AA.}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Model & Graph validity (\%) & Median force (eV/\AA) & Joint yield (\%)\\
\midrule
Gaussian source &44.53&3.404&35.68\\
Gaussian + paired update &46.61&3.222&40.63\\
Harmonic source &52.73&3.287&44.53\\
Harmonic + paired update &53.45&3.037&47.59\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\label{tab:absolute-quality}
\end{table}

Paired physical training raises GFN2 joint yield by 4.95 percentage points for
Gaussian (composition-bootstrap 95\% interval [2.86,7.10]) and 3.06 points for
harmonic ([1.04,5.14]), with positive changes in both continuations for both
sources. It also lowers the Gaussian model's paired, jointly valid energy by
0.01465 eV/atom and force RMS by 0.4618 eV/\AA. Thus the physical update benefits
both choices of source.

With physical training applied to both, harmonic improves pooled graph validity
by 6.84 points ([4.23,9.51]) and joint yield by 6.97 points ([3.26,10.61]).
The graph-validity difference is positive in both continuations; the joint-yield
differences are +16.28 and $-2.34$ points, showing substantial continuation
variation. Both within-source physical improvements and the pooled source
advantage also appear under eSEN. Figure~\ref{fig:primary-quality} in
Appendix~\ref{app:components} shows the complete force-threshold curves.
