\subsection{Absolute physical quality of raw generations}\label{sec:absolute-quality}
To measure usable output yield, we count samples that both pass the chemical-graph
assay and meet a chosen force-RMS threshold, then divide by every attempted output.
This retains invalid structures and failed energy calculations in the denominator.
Figure~\ref{fig:primary-quality} in Appendix~\ref{app:components} shows the
full threshold readout on the same
24-composition panel under both potentials.

At the 5 eV/\AA\ threshold, the paired physical update raises joint yield by
3.06 percentage points over the harmonic base under GFN2
(composition-bootstrap 95\% interval [1.04,5.21]). The increases are 3.13 and
2.99 points in the two continuations. Under eSEN the gain is 3.32 points
([1.11,5.60]). These are absolute quality readouts of already audited outputs;
the curves show how yield changes as the required residual force is tightened.
The physical update improves the fraction meeting a common force criterion
while preserving a similar graph-validity rate.
