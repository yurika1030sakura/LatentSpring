\subsection{Source and physical adaptation}\label{sec:absolute-quality}
We apply paired physical training to both source families, using generated
anchors from the same eight training compositions and matched maximum proposal
and training budgets. Table~\ref{tab:absolute-quality} compares all four models
on 24 compositions. Joint yield counts outputs that are graph-valid and have
GFN2 force RMS at most 5 eV/\AA, divided by all attempts. Appendix~\ref{sec:factorial-details}
records preparation costs and evaluation reuse.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Source and physical-update comparison under GFN2-xTB, with 1,536 raw
attempts per model. Median force RMS uses each model's own graph-valid outputs
with successful calculations. Joint yield uses all attempts and requires both
graph validity and force RMS at most 5 eV/\AA.}
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Model & Graph validity (\%) & Median force (eV/\AA) & Joint yield (\%)\\
\midrule
Gaussian source &44.53&3.404&35.68\\
Gaussian + paired update &46.61&3.222&40.63\\
Harmonic source &52.73&3.287&44.53\\
Harmonic + paired update &53.45&3.037&47.59\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\label{tab:absolute-quality}
\end{table}

The paired update improves GFN2 joint yield for both sources: by 4.95 percentage
points for Gaussian (composition-bootstrap 95\% interval [2.86,7.10]) and
3.06 points for harmonic ([1.04,5.14]). Both runs improve for both sources.
On paired, jointly valid outputs, the Gaussian update also lowers energy by
0.01465 eV/atom and force RMS by 0.4618 eV/\AA.

After physical adaptation, harmonic exceeds Gaussian by 6.84 points in pooled
graph validity ([4.23,9.51]) and 6.97 points in joint yield ([3.26,10.61]).
The validity difference is positive in both runs, but the joint-yield differences
are +16.28 and $-2.34$ points. Thus the pooled advantage coexists with substantial
variation between fine-tuning runs. eSEN also shows the within-source benefits of
physical adaptation and the pooled harmonic-source advantage.
Figure~\ref{fig:primary-quality} in Appendix~\ref{app:components} plots the
results across force thresholds.
