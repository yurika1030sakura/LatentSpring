\subsection{Repeated source training and budget dependence}\label{app:source-replication}
Three additional paired runs start from the same pretrained FlowMol checkpoint. Runs 3 and 5 use the first 3,000-row block; run 4 uses the second. Each pair changes only the source, sharing initial weights, reference order, and optimization. At 6,000 updates, the 3,000 rows have been visited twice. The study therefore measures stochastic fine-tuning variation on two fixed datasets and one pretrained checkpoint.

We evaluate every new run at 1,000, 3,000, and 6,000 updates on the existing 64-composition panel. The middle checkpoint uses 32 draws per composition, matching the original runs; the other two use 16. All checkpoints are reported.

At 3,000 updates, the five-run mean gain is $+3.89$ points with between-run sample standard deviation 5.27. The composition interval $[2.52,5.33]$ conditions on those fits. For the three new runs, composition intervals at increasing budgets are $[-4.04,0.68]$, $[0.36,3.52]$, and $[1.73,6.25]$. The source advantage thus depends on training duration and varies substantially across runs. The study adds 36,000 updates and 24,576 outputs, with no physical queries.
