\subsection{New compositions and independent physical evaluation}\label{sec:fresh-physics}
We evaluate frozen Gaussian, harmonic, force-updated harmonic, and complete-work-updated harmonic models on 24 new compositions. The force-only update was designated primary before the test. Fixed metadata ranks select eight compositions each with 17--20, 21--24, and 25--28 atoms, absent from both processed corpora (Appendix~\ref{app:data-evaluation}). The four methods produce 6,144 raw outputs.

Harmonic improves graph validity over Gaussian by 8.20 points (95\% CI $[5.08,11.33]$), with both runs improving. The force update changes harmonic validity by $+0.72$ points ($[-0.98,2.34]$). All accepted outputs have distinct inferred connectivities within composition.

\paragraph{Physical scores.}
We evaluate saved coordinates with mirror-averaged eSEN and independent gas-phase GFN2-xTB single-point energies and gradients \citep{gfn2xtb}. GFN2 is used only for evaluation. Four calculations fail among the 6,144 outputs, all graph-invalid; every graph-valid calculation and reference inversion check succeeds. Force RMS is the root mean squared atomic force magnitude.

The force update lowers energy and force RMS under both potentials on jointly valid pairs, with every composition contributing in both runs. The same directions hold over all eSEN outputs and all paired successful GFN2 calculations. Without physical adaptation, harmonic also lowers aggregate energy relative to Gaussian, mainly in one run.

Complete-work minus force-only energy is $-0.00413$ eV/atom under eSEN ($[-0.00903,0.00078]$) and $-0.00363$ under GFN2 ($[-0.00792,0.00075]$). Force and graph-validity intervals also include zero. We retain the cheaper force-only teacher because this comparison resolves no additional gain from work weighting.
