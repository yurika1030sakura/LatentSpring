\subsection{New compositions and independent physical evaluation}\label{sec:fresh-physics}
We next evaluate the frozen Gaussian, harmonic and two physical-update models on
24 new compositions. The force-only update is chosen as the primary physical
candidate before this evaluation. Starting from a pool of 256 distinct compositions
outside the earlier candidate sets, reference qualification accepts 121. Fixed
metadata ranks select eight compositions from each of the 17--20, 21--24 and
25--28 atom bins. All 24 are absent from the processed OMol25 training and
validation corpora. Reference coordinates are used solely for qualification.
Each model generates 32 samples per composition in each continuation, giving
6,144 raw outputs in total.

The harmonic source improves graph validity by 8.20 percentage points over
Gaussian (95\% CI $[5.08,11.33]$), with gains in both continuations. The force
update changes graph validity by $+0.72$ points relative to harmonic, with interval
$[-0.98,2.34]$, consistent with a similar support rate. Every accepted output has
distinct perceived connectivity within its composition in these draws.

\paragraph{Energy and force scores.}
Every output is evaluated at fixed coordinates with eSEN and gas-phase GFN2-xTB
single-point energies and gradients \citep{gfn2xtb}. GFN2 provides an independent
potential, used only for evaluation. Four of the 6,144 generated-geometry GFN2
calculations fail; all four concern graph-invalid outputs. Calculations succeed
for every graph-valid output, and reference inversion checks pass. Including
reference checks, evaluation costs 12,384 eSEN queries and 6,240 GFN2 attempts.
eSEN scores use the parity averages defined in Section~\ref{sec:flow}. Force
RMS is the square root of the mean squared atom-force norm.

The primary contrast uses paired outputs that pass the graph assay for both
models, with successful electronic calculations. Such pairs are available for all
24 compositions in both continuations. Energy and force improve under both
potentials. The same direction holds when including all outputs for eSEN and all
jointly successful calculations for GFN2. For the source-only harmonic--Gaussian
comparison, aggregate energy also decreases, with a larger effect in one
continuation than the other.

The complete-work and force-only updates perform similarly within sampling
uncertainty. Their energy difference among jointly valid outputs is $-0.00413$
eV/atom under eSEN, with interval $[-0.00903,0.00078]$, and $-0.00363$ under GFN2,
with interval $[-0.00792,0.00075]$. Force and graph differences also have intervals
spanning zero. The force-only update therefore offers comparable physical scores
with a lower teacher-preparation cost.
