\subsection{Independent backbone and generator objectives}\label{sec:matched-generators}
We train Gaussian FM, harmonic FM, position-conditional EDM, and GAGA
\citep{edm,qu2026gaga} from scratch on an independent EGNN. All four use the
same 20,000 OMol25 structures, architecture, and initial weights within each of
two repetitions, with 30,000 updates at batch size 32. This comparison omits
self-conditioning and physical adaptation. The two FM models differ only in
source distribution; their probability paths and atom pairing are identical.
Appendix~\ref{app:matched-generators} gives the protocol and GAGA configuration.

The harmonic source improves FM graph validity by 1.34 percentage points, with a
composition-bootstrap 95\% interval of [0.12,2.52]. This supports a source benefit
without shared pretraining history. EDM and GAGA achieve higher validity in this
setting. Unlike the Gaussian--harmonic FM comparison, those comparisons also
change the probability path, training objective, and sampler.

Adding distance self-conditioning to harmonic FM gives 15.63\% validity versus
13.77\% for validation-selected GAGA on a separate 32-composition panel, at the
same training forward-pass budget. The difference is $+1.86$ points with a
composition interval of $[-0.34,4.44]$; this comparison does not establish a
reliable ordering. Appendix~\ref{app:feedback-transfer} reports the selection,
feedback controls, and physical-update transfer experiment.

The learned physical head transfers to both EGNN generators on another 16 new
compositions. At matched physical-supervision budgets, FM joint yield rises from
8.01\% to 15.04\% and GAGA from 10.94\% to 17.58\%. Their paired improvements
are $+7.03$ points ($[2.73,11.91]$) and $+6.64$ points ($[3.71,9.77]$),
respectively, with both fits improving. The ordering between the corrected
generators remains uncertain (Appendix~\ref{app:matched-connection}).

A further 16-composition comparison evaluates complete systems with their own
architectures and training histories. The paired-update model reaches 38.09\%
GFN2 joint yield, versus 9.77\% and 12.11\% for GAGA at 128 and 651 calls.
Learned force correction gives 46.88\%, compared with 38.87\% for its frozen
parent. Appendix~\ref{app:common-models} reports all variants, preparation costs,
and the matched-size and training-duration controls. These complete-system
results complement the architecture-controlled experiments above.
