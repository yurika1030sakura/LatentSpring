\section{Additional source results}\label{app:additional-source-results}
\subsection{Effect of the latent spring source}
Replacing the isotropic Gaussian source with the harmonic source improves
chemical-graph validity by 5.86 percentage points on the 12 development
compositions, 8.83 points on the 10 additional compositions, and 8.20 points on
the 24-composition set. These comparisons hold the backbone, initialization, and
training data fixed. A Gaussian with approximately matched covariance also
performs worse. This supports a benefit beyond the tested covariance approximation,
without isolating every property of the mixture. Appendix~\ref{app:source-panels}
reports the source, shell-tree, and covariance controls.

\paragraph{Independent physical evaluation of weight updates.}
On 24 new compositions, paired physical training lowers energy and force RMS
under both the eSEN teacher and the independent GFN2 evaluator. Graph validity
remains similar. Appendix~\ref{sec:fresh-physics} gives all-attempt counts,
paired valid-output comparisons, and the complete-work control.
\paragraph{Learning physical feedback in weights.}
The paired update also lowers energy and force relative to a direct update of
the same parameter norm. Both fitted models favor the paired direction.
Appendix~\ref{sec:update-ablation} reports this control, and
Appendix~\ref{sec:absolute-quality} compares physical adaptation under both
source distributions.

\paragraph{Component controls.}
At matched training-forward budgets, self-conditioning improves validity by
12.43 points ($[9.70,15.23]$). Removing the force displacement raises energy by
0.01939 eV/atom on paired graph-valid outputs. Appendix~\ref{app:components}
reports both runs, validity, force scores, and the update-matched comparison.
