\paragraph{Connectivity and geometry of individual outputs.}
The first four distinct graph-valid outputs for the first eligible CHON composition in run 1 of the paired-update study have two, two, two, and five independent cycles. The examples were selected in data order without energy ranking or geometry optimization. Only one passes the stricter geometric screen in Appendix~\ref{app:chemical-geometry}; the others fail angle, planarity, or internal-distance checks. The release contains their coordinates and individual diagnostics.
