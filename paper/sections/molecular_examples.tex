\paragraph{Connectivity and geometry of individual outputs.}
For the paired-update variant, the first four graph-valid outputs with distinct
connectivities from the first eligible CHON composition in run 1 contain two,
two, two, and five independent cycles. These archived examples were selected
without energy ranking or geometry optimization. One passes the additional
geometry screen in Appendix~\ref{app:chemical-geometry}; the others fail angle,
planarity, or internal-distance checks. The per-example checks and original
coordinates are included with the released evidence. Figure~\ref{fig:method}
shows a separate raw output that passes the stated geometric checks, together
with its inferred two-dimensional connectivity.
