\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Energy values from a transferable universal potential are usable supervision
for a de novo molecular generator. One group-wise variance term, costing pre-computed labels and no
partition function, raises held-out local density--energy ordering from $0.200$ to $0.369$ and top-1
retrieval from $20.2\%$ to $29.6\%$ under an independent scorer, and a matched grid places that gain
in the correct geometry--energy correspondence rather than in an auxiliary penalty.

The same analysis bounds what it buys. What we measure is a finite-step estimate of the clamped
positional-flow density $q_\theta$, which need not equal the generator's joint conditional
$p_\theta(x \mid c)$ (\Cref{ass:A2}); the
objective is also numerically demanding, so every value-cell mean is conditional on completion and
carries its launched, completed and diverged counts (\Cref{app:stability}). And it is local: inside
a group the density--energy relation is fixed only up to one constant per connected component and
our groups are disconnected (\Cref{prop:ident}), so relative basin mass is not identified and raw
calibration is not achieved (\Cref{app:limitations} has the full inventory). Local ordering,
calibration and global allocation are three targets rather than one, and we deliver the first with a
design criterion for the rest --- connect the groups, or add a global term.
