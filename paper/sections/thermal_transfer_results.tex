\paragraph{Transferring the paired physical update.}
We use parameter arithmetic \citep{ilharco2023arithmetic} to transfer the difference
between physical and replay continuations from the same initialization:

\begin{equation}
 \theta_{\rm new}=\theta_{\rm frozen}
   +(\theta_{\rm physical}-\theta_{\rm replay}),
\end{equation}
for both unweighted-escort and complete-work students. The coefficient is fixed
at one. Parameter tensors are updated, while buffers and the source distribution
remain fixed.

Both physical-update variants lower energy and force in both continuations.
The work-difference variant reduces force RMS by 0.38403 eV/\AA\ on common graph

also improve. Its observed graph-rate difference is $-0.55$ percentage points,
with interval $[-2.58,1.56]$.

The two physical-update variants have similar scores: their common-supported
energy difference is $-0.00243$ eV/atom, with
interval $[-0.00756,0.00264]$, while all-attempt energy difference is $+0.00022$
with interval $[-0.00557,0.00649]$. Force and graph intervals also span zero.
The unweighted variant requires 702 anchor queries for teacher preparation,
compared with 6,232 for complete work. Both retain the same 128-call neural
inference procedure.

Evaluating the two transferred variants adds 2,560 generated outputs and 5,120
physical queries. The parameter transfer itself requires no optimizer steps.
We use these development results to choose the force-only update for the
new-composition evaluation in Section~\ref{sec:fresh-physics}.
