\paragraph{Separating the paired update from continuation regression.}
The failed direct continuation motivates one subsequent fixed-coefficient
parameter-arithmetic test. Parameter arithmetic is established
\citep{ilharco2023arithmetic}; we make no claim to invent it or to subtract
functional drift exactly. For the same initialization and paired training draws,
construct
\begin{equation}
 \theta_{\rm new}=\theta_{\rm frozen}
   +(\theta_{\rm physical}-\theta_{\rm replay}),
\end{equation}
for both unweighted-escort and complete-work students. The coefficient is one;
there is no coefficient, layer-selection or extra-training sweep. Only parameter
tensors change, while all buffers and the source distribution remain fixed.
All four reconstructed checkpoints are checked tensor by tensor and restore.

This exploratory intervention passes the frozen-baseline physical-improvement
gate for work transfer, but fails the separate extra-work-over-escort gate.
It adds2,560 generated outputs and5,120 physical queries. No new optimizer steps
are used in the transfer itself. Existing panel outcomes informed the choice of
intervention, so independent composition confirmation and physical-model
validation remain necessary. These results support investigating the simpler
physics update while retaining complete work as a defined and tested comparison;
they do not establish global Boltzmann sampling or new task-arithmetic theory.
