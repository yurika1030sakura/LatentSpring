\paragraph{Paired physical update.}
For both uniform-escort and complete-work students, we subtract the replay update through parameter arithmetic \citep{ilharco2023arithmetic}:
\begin{equation}
 \theta_{\rm final}=\theta_{\rm base}
   +(\theta_{\rm physical}-\theta_{\rm replay}),
\end{equation}
The coefficient is one, and buffers and the source distribution remain fixed.
