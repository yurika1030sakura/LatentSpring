\section{Combined source and correction transfer to diffusion}\label{app:joint-design-transfer}
\paragraph{Extending the source to diffusion.}
To transfer both designs, we use the harmonic mixture as structured diffusion
noise. For a sampled tree $T$, let $B_T$ map independent standard Gaussian edge
vectors to centered coordinates with the scales in Equation~\ref{eq:edge-law}.
Its coordinate covariance is $C_T=B_TB_T^\top$. A forward diffusion state is
\begin{equation}
 X_t=\alpha_t X_{\rm clean}+\sigma_t B_T\Xi,
 \qquad \Xi\in\RR^{(N-1)\times3},\quad \Xi_{ij}\sim\mathcal N(0,1).
 \label{eq:structured-diffusion}
\end{equation}
The tree stays fixed along a trajectory, and reverse-sampling noise uses the
same covariance. The denoiser receives invariant pair variances from $C_T$ and
predicts the noise $B_T\Xi$ in coordinate units. This follows the established
non-isotropic Gaussian diffusion construction \citep{voleti2022nonisotropic}.
The target backbone is trained for this noise process; the FM physical head
is then reused through the same endpoint correction. Appendix~\ref{app:main-implementation}
gives the covariance conditioning and GAGA initialization.


The combined-design experiment trains tree-conditioned diffusion models using
Equation~\ref{eq:structured-diffusion}, then attaches the frozen FM correction
head. EDM and GAGA each use two original initialization seeds, the same 20,000
OMol25 rows and batch schedules as their baselines, and 30,000 updates per fit.
The source covariance is held fixed along each trajectory and supplied through
an invariant edge channel. Every model uses its final EMA checkpoint, 128
backbone evaluations per sample, correction strength four, and no hydrogen
readout. The baselines reuse their audited original outputs on the same
64 compositions. Each setting contains 2,048 attempts.

The adaptation reduces generation quality in both target families. Its two
GAGA fits produce one and four graph-valid outputs, respectively; only one
also passes the force threshold. The EDM fits produce no graph-valid outputs.
There are 36 unsuccessful GFN2 calculations for EDM and 21 for GAGA; all remain
in the denominators. The experiment contributes 120,000 backbone updates,
3.84 million training-example forwards, 4,096 generated trajectories and GFN2
attempts, with no new head training or force-teacher queries. The released
protocol, training logs, arrays and audit retain every fit.
