\subsection{Comparison with generative baselines}\label{sec:main-comparison}
We compare LatentSpring with Gaussian-source flow matching, EDM, and GAGA.
LatentSpring combines the harmonic-mixture source, geometric recovery, and
learned endpoint corrections; the baselines use their own generators without these additions.
Figure~\ref{fig:main-generators} reports graph, force and geometric criteria on the same compositions.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../research/figures/geometric_results_v1/main_comparison.pdf}
\caption{Generation quality on 64 unseen compositions. (A) Open markers show graph
validity; filled markers show joint yield at the 5 eV/\AA\ force threshold. The
connecting segment counts graph-valid outputs above that threshold. (B) Empirical
geometry-and-force yield curves computed from all attempts; the dashed line marks
5 eV/\AA. Colors and line styles identify the same methods in both panels. Gaussian FM and EDM
use two fits (2,048 outputs per method); GAGA and LatentSpring use five (5,120).
The hydrogen readout is disabled.}\label{fig:main-generators}
\end{figure}

LatentSpring reaches 35.49\% graph validity and 35.35\% joint graph-and-force
yield. The latter compares with 8.75\% for GAGA, 8.15\% for EDM, and 6.25\%
for Gaussian FM. The corresponding gains are 26.60, 27.20, and 29.10 percentage
points. Requiring the additional geometric checks gives 9.63\% geometry-and-force
yield, compared with 2.40\% for GAGA, 2.83\% for EDM, and 1.51\% for Gaussian FM.

Across all five fits, geometry-and-force yield rises from 4.43\% for the
initial force-corrected FM to 9.63\% for the complete model. The three additional
fits each improve, with a mean gain of 5.08 points and crossed fit/composition
95\% interval $[3.09,7.52]$. In the two-fit matched-training comparison, the
complete model gives 10.25\%, versus 5.76\% for a continuation control with
the same number of backbone updates. The 4.49-point difference has crossed
interval $[2.44,6.45]$. Appendix~\ref{app:geometric-recovery} gives the controls
and Appendix~\ref{app:chemical-geometry} reports the geometric criteria.

On a complementary panel of 12 common-element organic compositions, the same
model reaches 35.42\% geometry-and-force yield, versus 20.57\% for the initial
force-corrected FM, 17.71\% for GAGA, 14.84\% for EDM, and 16.41\% for Gaussian
FM. The gain over the initial FM is 14.84 points, with crossed interval
$[7.81,25.52]$. Appendix~\ref{app:chemical-geometry} gives the selection rule,
all 384 attempts per method, and inferred-charge diagnostics.
