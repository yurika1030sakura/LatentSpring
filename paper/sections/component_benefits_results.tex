\subsection{Self-conditioning}\label{sec:sc-ablation}
We remove self-conditioning while retaining the harmonic model's initialization, 3,000 references, source, and pairing procedure. One single-pass model receives 3,000 updates; another receives 6,000 to match the self-conditioned training-forward count. Both use 64 midpoint steps, so all variants sample with 128 network calls.

Self-conditioning adds 7.23 graph-validity points at matched updates (95\% interval [4.69,9.77]) and 12.43 at matched forward counts ([9.70,15.23]), with both runs improving. Additional single-pass training therefore does not recover the gain in these runs. Forward counts are matched rather than runtime. Section~\ref{sec:update-ablation} gives the update-direction control.

\paragraph{Force displacement.}
We remove the force shift while retaining Gaussian perturbations, candidate indices, force-dependent widths, selection, training schedule, and paired parameter update. Relative to this control, shifted targets lower jointly valid paired energy by $0.01939$ eV/atom (signed-change 95\% interval [-0.02417,-0.01455]) and force RMS by $0.3926$ eV/\AA\ ([-0.4696,-0.3263]). Both runs improve. Graph validity changes by $+0.39$ points ([-1.33,2.19]). The comparison isolates the displacement, with other force-dependent target-construction choices retained.
