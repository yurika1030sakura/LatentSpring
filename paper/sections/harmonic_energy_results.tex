\paragraph{Physical scores of frozen source models.}
We evaluate every output and reference on the 10-composition additional panel with the same conservative eSEN checkpoint, preserving charge and spin. Inversion averages are $E_+(X)=[E(X)+E(-X)]/2$ and $F_+(X)=[F(X)-F(-X)]/2$. Differences are paired within composition. Subtracting each composition's reference energy per atom leaves the paired model difference unchanged.
