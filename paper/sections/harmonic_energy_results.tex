\paragraph{Physical evaluation of the frozen source models.}
This evaluation concerns the pretrained source models on the 10-composition
additional test set. We score every generated output and its original reference
with the same conservative eSEN checkpoint at the original charge and spin.
Energies and forces are averaged over inversions as
$E_+(X)=[E(X)+E(-X)]/2$ and $F_+(X)=[F(X)-F(-X)]/2$.
We pair differences within composition before averaging. Per-atom energies also
subtract the composition's reference energy; that reference cancels in a paired
model difference. The evaluation uses 5,160 raw energy/force queries, including
both inversions of references in each fine-tuning run.

For each sample, force RMS is the square root of the mean squared atomic force
norm; we then average this value over samples. The jointly valid comparison
retains only pairs accepted under both models, with 3--35 pairs in each
composition/run combination. The all-attempt comparison also includes graph-invalid
outputs. Reporting both distinguishes overall scores from scores on pairs that
remain valid under both methods.

Most of the aggregate improvement comes from run 1. The all-attempt energy
differences are $-0.08049$ and $-0.00070$ eV/atom in runs 1 and 2, respectively;
the force-RMS differences are $-1.21996$ and $+0.29302$ eV/\AA.
