\section{Physical feedback through paired training targets}\label{app:paired-training}
A frozen harmonic generator supplies graph-valid anchors $A$ on training compositions excluded from evaluation. Around each anchor, we sample $X\sim q_A=\mathcal N_{\HH_N}(A,\sigma_A^2I)$ in centered coordinate space.

The eSEN teacher supplies mirror-averaged energy $E_+$ and force $F_+$ (Appendix~\ref{app:local-work}). We translate each candidate by $Y=X+\delta_A$, with $\delta_A=\sigma_A^2F_+(A)/(k_B T_{\rm phys})$ at $T_{\rm phys}=300$ K. Under a locally linear energy, this \emph{force escort} exactly shifts the Gaussian to its Boltzmann-reweighted mean. The width $\sigma_A$ is fixed before candidate sampling to bound the displacement. We then sample uniformly from candidates that pass geometric checks and preserve the anchor's inferred labeled graph.

Two copies of $\theta_{\rm base}$ follow the same fine-tuning schedule and share OMol25 references. The physical student learns from shifted targets, while the replay student learns from the original anchors. Their difference removes the matched replay update:
\begin{equation}
 \theta_{\rm final} = \theta_{\rm base} + (\theta_{\rm physical}-\theta_{\rm replay}).
 \label{eq:physical-transfer}
\end{equation}
The elementwise parameter arithmetic \citep{ilharco2023arithmetic}, with coefficient one, produces a single adapted network. Section~\ref{sec:update-ablation} and Appendix~\ref{app:local-work} compare it with direct fine-tuning and a norm-matched update.

\paragraph{Work-weighted targets.}
Complete-work weights account for energy, local Gaussian density, and coordinate-map volume change. Appendices~\ref{app:jarzynski}--\ref{app:rotor-work} derive the weights and test recovery of specified local distributions. The primary paired-update model instead uses the less expensive uniformly sampled force-shifted targets.
