\section{Capped conditional proposal proof}
Fix a terminal/anchor pair and context $c$. Let $h_c(v)$ be the base angular
proposal density and let its internal acceptance satisfy
$h_c(v)a_c(v)=C(c){\bf1}_{D(c)}(v)\exp s_\theta(v;c)$, with $C(c)>0$ independent
of $v$. Let $Z(c)=\int h_c(v)a_c(v)\,d\Omega(v)$. Independence of successive trials
gives the non-self density after a cap $K$ as
\[
 Q_K(v\mid c)=h_c(v)a_c(v)\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}(1-Z(c))^j.
\]
The probability of exhausting the cap is $(1-Z(c))^K$ and yields a self-transition.
For two eligible directions with the same context, all factors except their
unnormalized angular scores cancel. Including the selection probability of the
leaf/anchor pair yields Eq.~\eqref{eq:angular-mh}. The usual minimum probability
flow then gives detailed balance. The chart determinant on $\HH$ is constant,
and the common radius contributes the same $r^2$ factor in each direction.

This argument does not apply if the guide, support or cap depends on the old
direction through an unmasked feature. In that case the factors involving
$C$, $Z$ or $K$ can differ and must enter the reverse ratio. Our finite-state
negative control exhibits the resulting bias when context invariance is broken.

\section{Directional envelope proof}
Let $t=|v^\top u|\in[0,1]$, $d=\lambda_3-\lambda_2\ge0$. The spectral bound gives
$u^\top A u\le\lambda_2+d t^2$. Since the derivative of $\log\cosh z$ is bounded
by one for $z\ge0$,
\[
 \log\cosh d-\log\cosh(dt)\le d(1-t).
\]
Consequently
\[
 u^\top A u-\log\cosh(dt)-[\lambda_3-\log\cosh d]
 \le-d(1-t^2)+d(1-t)=-dt(1-t)\le0.
\]
For natural parameters $\eta_\pm=\eta\pm dv$, let $C_\pm$ be their vMF normalizers.
Mixture weights proportional to $C_\pm^{-1}$ produce a normalized base density
proportional to $\exp(\eta^\top u)\cosh(dv^\top u)$. Multiplying by the bounded
rejection probability gives a constant times $\exp(\eta^\top u+u^\top A u)$.
This constant is fixed by the invariant context and therefore cancels in the
capped conditional MH construction.

For identical coefficients and any common support, let $Z_D=\int_D\exp(s_\theta)
\,d\Omega$. Uniform score rejection succeeds per trial with probability
$Z_D/(4\pi e^M)$, whereas the two-vMF envelope succeeds with probability
$Z_D/(H e^B)$, where $H$ is the normalizing integral of the base mixture's
unnormalized score. Their exact ratio is $4\pi e^M/(H e^B)$; no support volume
or $Z_D$ estimate is needed. Since
$H\le4\pi e^{\|\eta\|}\cosh d$ and $B=\lambda_3-\log\cosh d$, this ratio
is at least one for $M=\|\eta\|+\lambda_3$.
On identical saved development contexts, median per-trial factors are183.6/265.4
for the two tensor models and86.4/85.7 for vector models. These are internal
rejection-success factors, not runtime speedups or reductions in equilibrium error.

\section{Implementation verification and interpretation}
Tests check masked-context equality under leaf motion and COM recentering,
orthogonal and permutation covariance, batch independence, surface derivatives,
rejection envelopes, normalized vMF densities and known supported S2 targets.
The original molecular paths retain paired raw forces and energies, every
candidate trial and all random streams. Frozen-producer replay exactly reproduces
the first learned study's coordinates, and a separate calculation checks the
physical-energy and angular-score acceptance ratio.

These checks verify the specified implementation and its mathematical contracts.
They do not independently validate the energy model, establish quantum chemical
validity, estimate a source likelihood or prove finite-time convergence to
Eq.~\eqref{eq:angular-target}. Those scientific limits remain explicit in the main
text and must be addressed before submission.
