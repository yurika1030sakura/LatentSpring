\subsection{Complete-work correction on molecular rotors}\label{sec:rotor-work}
For each of the three rotors above, a 512-point GFN2 interpolation defines $U(\phi)$. At 300 K, the target is $\rho(\phi)\propto q_0(\phi)\exp[-(U(\phi)-U(0))/kT]$, where $kT=k_B T_{\rm phys}$ and $q_0$ is the concentration-one von Mises reference. The target retains this angular restraint while reweighting by energy.

For each of the three maps, we compare complete-work, energy-only, no-Jacobian, and unweighted estimates. Complete work accounts for both reference-density change and interval volume. Each setting uses 128 repetitions at sample counts from 8 to 2,048 (Appendix~\ref{app:rotor-work}).

\caption{Work correction for restrained molecular rotors at 300 K. (a) Reference structures and methyl rotations. (b,c) Angular-distribution and normalizer errors averaged over three molecules and two nonidentity maps, with 128 repetitions per case. Unweighted samples have no free-energy estimator; all targets retain their angular reference restraint.}\label{fig:rotor-work}

At 2,048 samples, complete work gives lower mean total-variation error than either incomplete weighting in all six nonidentity cases. Mean errors are 0.0636, 0.1532, and 0.1134 for complete work, energy-only, and no-Jacobian weighting, respectively: a 58.5\% reduction relative to energy-only weights. Free-energy RMSE ranges from 0.158 to 0.734 meV. Independent density-ratio quadrature recovers the normalizer in all nine molecule/map cases. For the identity map, all three weighted methods coincide. These tests establish recovery of the specified restrained rotor distributions.
