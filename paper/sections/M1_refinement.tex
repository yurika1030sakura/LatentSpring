\begin{abstract}
Energy refinement of a pretrained molecular generator requires accounting for
changes in its distribution, although its marginal density and score may be
unavailable. We study a frozen-generator construction that learns a separate
invertible map using physical energy and exact changes in log volume. The
candidate architecture decomposes Cartesian coordinates into element-group
internal displacements and element-centroid differences. Invariant neural
contexts condition nonlinear point maps whose centered Jacobian determinants
require only three-dimensional matrix factorizations. Standard change of
variables gives an estimable relative endpoint-KL objective without the source
entropy or target normalizer. On one eight-atom development condition, two
training streams show small improvements over typed-linear and matched
neural-context affine controls. The latter differences are
$-0.0133\pm0.0025$ and $-0.0126\pm0.0021$ nat on shared confirmation samples.
Path importance weights remain severely concentrated. A broader eight-condition
comparison is specified and launched, but its performance results and external
flow comparisons are not yet available. We therefore present an implemented
method candidate and its limitations, without claiming calibrated Boltzmann
sampling or an established novel-method advantage.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Molecular generators can propose three-dimensional configurations without
running an equilibrium simulation. Flow matching offers a flexible way to
learn such generators \citep{lipman2023flowmatching,flowmol3}. Adapting their
outputs to a specified energy target, however, requires more than lowering
potential energy: concentrating a distribution also changes its entropy.
An implicit generator need not expose the marginal density or score of its
actual sampling procedure. An inaccurate surrogate for either quantity can
change the objective while retaining a plausible energy narrative.

We investigate a modular alternative. Keep the source generator fixed and learn
an invertible map of its outputs. The entropy change then depends on the map's
Jacobian, while the unknown source entropy is constant. This is established
normalizing-flow and Boltzmann-generator reasoning \citep{noe2019boltzmann}.
The architectural question is how to obtain useful, symmetry-preserving molecular
refinement with tractable volume accounting and without an additional
marginal-density approximation.

Our candidate uses atomic-number groups to define conditional coordinate
updates. An invariant message network predicts parameters of a strongly monotone
point map. Centering these point updates preserves each group's centroid, with
an explicit correction to the volume on the constrained space. Additional
centroid-pair updates move element groups relative to one another. The method
acts directly on Cartesian coordinates and requires no supervised bond labels.
The setting is conditional refinement given composition, charge and spin, using
OMol25 as the primary data source \citep{omol25}.

The current contribution is an implemented construction and a controlled
development study. We provide a constrained-volume derivation, actual interface
and gradient checks, two training replicas and a matched architectural ablation.
These establish numerical consistency and a small relative-KL signal on one
condition. They do not establish a calibrated general sampler, broad
generalization or sufficient novelty for a completed method paper. The broader
comparison and remaining scientific gates are stated explicitly below.

\paragraph{Relation to prior methods.}
Equivariant finite flows and augmented coupling flows already provide
symmetry-preserving invertible models \citep{bose2021equivariantfinite,midgley2023eacf}.
Convex-potential and residual flows supply related invertibility principles
\citep{huang2020convex,chen2019residual}. Our candidate distinction is the
element-group decomposition and nonlinear centered point transformation;
equivariance, the KL objective and the linear-algebra identities themselves
are not new claims. External performance comparison remains necessary.

\section{Conditional source and physical target}
For fixed atomic numbers $z_1,\ldots,z_n$, charge $Q$ and spin multiplicity $S$,
let
\[
 H=\{x\in\mathbb R^{n\times3}:\textstyle\sum_i x_i=0\},
 \qquad d=3(n-1).
\]
The measure on $H$ is induced by an orthonormal basis. The centroid uses atom
counts, matching this declared measure; it is not a mass-weighted dynamical
coordinate. Coordinates retain atom labels, and the construction is equivariant
under joint permutations of coordinates and element identities.

The target is
\begin{equation}
 \pi(x)=Z^{-1}\exp[-U(x)/(kT)],\qquad
 U(x)=E_{\mathrm{eSEN}}(x;z,Q,S)+\frac{\kappa}{2}\sum_i\|x_i\|^2.
 \label{eq:refine-target}
\end{equation}
The reported cold-target studies use $kT=0.025851999786435$ eV and
$\kappa=0.1$ eV/$\mathrm{\mathring A}^2$. The learned potential, confinement
and electronic state are part of the target. This is not an unconfined
experimental or direct-DFT ensemble. Artificial flow time is not molecular
dynamics time, and an energy histogram also depends on the density of states.

The source $q_0$ is frozen throughout adapter training. We require samples,
but neither a source-density evaluator nor an estimated source score. The
one-condition study uses a previously trained finite Gaussian-path generator.
The new broader study uses a different source: a fixed 64-step molecular FM
map followed by independent COM-Gaussian noise of standard deviation 0.025
$\mathrm{\mathring A}$. This source uses a separately trained displacement
head with fixed atom identities and electronic state; history-dependent
self-conditioning and steric retractions do not enter this map.
Its original 1-eV neural temperature input remains
fixed and is distinct from the 300-K physical target. No reference coordinates
initialize either source. Comparisons must identify the source law explicitly
rather than pool these different experiments.

\begin{assumption}[Scope of the entropy identity]
The target normalizer is finite, $q_0$ has a density on $H$, and the entropies
and energy expectations in the stated KL differences are finite. The learned
map is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of $H$.
\label{ass:refinement}
\end{assumption}
Positive terminal Gaussian noise ensures a positive source density for the
broader construction; it does not by itself certify target integrability or
distributional calibration.

\section{Neural couplings on element groups}
For each atomic-number group $g$, write $n_g$ for its size,
$\mu_g=n_g^{-1}\sum_{i\in g}x_i$ and $u_i=x_i-\mu_g$. The coordinate space
splits into group-internal subspaces and a centroid space satisfying
$\sum_g n_g\mu_g=0$.

\paragraph{Contexts held fixed by a layer.}
For an internal-group update, replace the active group's coordinates by
$\mu_g$ when constructing the neural context. For a centroid-pair update
on groups $g,h$, preserve their internal displacements but replace both
centroids by $m=(n_g\mu_g+n_h\mu_h)/(n_g+n_h)$. All other coordinates remain
available. These contexts are independent of the active internal displacement
or active centroid difference. Thus the layer is triangular in active/passive
coordinates. Context derivatives are retained when differentiating the full
model; triangularity does not justify detaching them during training.

The shared conditioner has two invariant message-passing blocks, hidden width 32,
24 radial features, element and role embeddings, and inputs for charge, spin,
log target temperature and layer mode. Scalar outputs parameterize the point
map; relative context vectors supply equivariant directions and a bounded
centroid shift. Final heads are zero-initialized, giving exact identity.
Neither dropout nor batch-coupled normalization is used.

\paragraph{Bounded nonlinear point map.}
With fixed context, define
\begin{equation}
 F(u)=\lambda u+\sum_{j=1}^{M}a_j\operatorname{sigmoid}(b_j^\top u+c_j)b_j
       +a_r\frac{u}{\sqrt{\ell^2+\|u\|^2}}.
 \label{eq:point-map}
\end{equation}
Use $\lambda=\exp[0.25\tanh(s)]$, $\|b_j\|\leq1/\ell$,
$|a_j|\leq2\beta\lambda\ell^2/M$ and
$|a_r|\leq\beta\lambda\ell/2$, with $\beta = 0.25$.
These bounds imply $\|DF-\lambda I\|\leq\beta\lambda$.
Although individual coefficients can be signed, the total potential whose
gradient is $F$ remains strongly convex. On an internal group, use
\begin{equation}
 T_i(u)=F(u_i)-\frac1{n_g}\sum_{j\in g}F(u_j).
 \label{eq:centered-map}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Constrained volume and reconstruction]
Let $A_i=DF(u_i)$ and let $H_g$ be the zero-sum group subspace. The map in
\eqref{eq:centered-map} is invertible on $H_g$, with
\begin{equation}
 \log\det_{H_g}DT
 =\sum_{i\in g}\log\det A_i+
   \log\det\!\left(\frac1{n_g}\sum_{i\in g}A_i^{-1}\right).
 \label{eq:centered-volume}
\end{equation}
The iteration $u^{+}=\lambda^{-1}[y-P_g(F(u)-\lambda u)]$ contracts by at
most $\beta$, where $P_g$ removes the group mean.
\label{prop:centered-volume}
\end{proposition}
The last determinant in \eqref{eq:centered-volume} is essential: multiplying
ambient point determinants alone gives the wrong constrained volume.
Only $3\times3$ matrix factorizations are required for this identity. The
conditioner still uses complete-graph interactions, so this is not a claim of
linear overall network complexity. Appendix \ref{app:refinement-proofs}
derives the result using standard Schur-complement and contraction arguments.
The numerical inverse is used for reconstruction checks; forward training
does not differentiate through the inverse iteration.

\paragraph{Centroid motion and composition.}
For active $\delta=\mu_g-\mu_h$, apply $\delta'=F(\delta)+t$ and set
\[
 \mu_g'=m+\frac{n_h}{n_g+n_h}\delta',\qquad
 \mu_h'=m-\frac{n_g}{n_g+n_h}\delta'.
\]
The shift $t$ is equivariant and bounded by 0.1 $\mathrm{\mathring A}$.
Internal displacements stay fixed, total COM is preserved, and the layer's
log volume is $\log\det DF(\delta)$. The fixed count-dependent coordinate
change cancels in the determinant. A sweep applies internal-group updates
for groups of size at least two, followed by adjacent element-centroid pairs
in atomic-number order. This avoids sorting by coordinates or arbitrary atom IDs.
The eight-atom model has seven layers and 19,365 parameters. Small or homogeneous
contexts restrict angular expressivity; the construction is not claimed universal.

\section{Training with exact entropy change}
\begin{proposition}[Relative endpoint KL]
Under Assumption \ref{ass:refinement},
\begin{equation}
 \KL(T_\#q_0\Vert\pi)-\KL(q_0\Vert\pi)
 =\mathbb E_{x\sim q_0}\left[
   \frac{U(Tx)-U(x)}{kT}-\log\det_H DT(x)\right].
 \label{eq:relative-kl}
\end{equation}
\label{prop:relative-kl}
\end{proposition}
This is a standard change-of-variables identity. It motivates minimizing
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L(\theta)=\mathbb E_{q_0}\left[
     U(T_\theta x)/(kT)-\log\det_H DT_\theta(x)\right].
 \label{eq:refinement-loss}
\end{equation}
Physical forces supply the gradient of the energy term through the complete
forward map. The volume term is differentiated analytically through the
implementation. The unknown source entropy and target normalizer cancel;
neither an estimated score nor an approximate CNF trace is substituted.
Unfreezing the source would change its entropy and invalidate this objective
as a complete gradient for the combined generator.

When a source has valid explicit path work $W$, carrying its auxiliary
variables through the endpoint inverse gives
$W'=W+[U(Tx)-U(x)]/(kT)-\log\det_HDT(x)$. The conditional auxiliary gap then
stays fixed. This supports exact work increments in the one-condition study,
but neither the identity nor a mean improvement guarantees useful importance
weights. No such path density has been evaluated for the broader FM-plus-noise
source, and no importance ESS is assigned to it.

\paragraph{Matched controls.}
The typed-linear control uses $T(x)=\exp(K)x$,
$K=[W-\operatorname{diag}(W\mathbf1)]/n$, with symmetric type-pair weights
bounded by 0.25; its intrinsic log volume is $3\operatorname{tr}K$.
The stronger affine-context control retains the complete neural conditioner
and replaces each active point map by $F(0)+DF(0)u$. Its full conditioned
composition remains nonlinear. Centering cancels $F(0)$ in internal layers,
whose log volume becomes $(n_g-1)\log\det DF(0)$; centroid-pair layers retain
the offset. This ablation isolates the active point transformation under the
fixed training recipe, rather than conflating it with neural context capacity.

\section{Completed evidence and broader experiment}
\paragraph{Numerical qualification.}
The full-model checks use nonzero parameters and include complete intrinsic
Jacobians, context invariance, inverse composition, identity initialization,
joint coordinate/element permutation, O(3) equivariance, independent batch
evaluation and parameter finite differences. Reconstruction is exercised up
to 200 atoms. Fourteen model tests cover both neural families. The complete
repository suite currently passes 289 tests, including source-contract corruption
and oracle-accounting checks. These are implementation checks, not evidence
of molecular sampling quality. Trained checkpoints also undergo inverse,
dense-Jacobian and public-loader replay checks.

\paragraph{One-condition training replication.}
The completed study conditions on $\mathrm{C_2H_2MgO_2P}$, charge $+1$ and
singlet multiplicity, on the labelled eight-atom space. Every arm receives
the same 4,096 training parents, 1,000 updates and batch 16. Each training run
uses 16,512 physical queries, including 256-row base and adapted development
evaluation. Neural models use a cosine learning rate from $0.001$ to $0.00001$;
the 13-parameter typed-linear control uses $0.01$ to $0.0001$.
The two neural families have identical parameter counts, initialization and
learning-rate schedules within a matched training pair. A second pair changes
neural initialization and minibatch-selection seeds. There is no best-checkpoint
selection. The preceding 200-step screen showed no clear convex advantage over
typed linear ($-0.0236\pm0.0258$ nat) and remains in the development record.

\paragraph{The retained sampling limitation.}
The base path ESS is 1.0039/2,048. Convex ESS is 1.0028 and 1.0016; typed-linear
ESS is 1.0036 and 1.0033. The second affine-context model has ESS 1.0014.
Thus the relative-KL signal does not repair this importance-sampling failure.
Low path ESS alone also does not identify the absolute endpoint KL, because
it includes the conditional auxiliary mismatch. Global probability calibration
remains unestablished.

Independent GFN2-xTB optimization converges for all 32 fixed attempts in the
base, typed and convex arms. Base/typed/convex median strains are
0.6205/0.5981/0.5934 eV for pair 1 and 0.6205/0.5980/0.5913 for pair 2.
These success-only medians accompany all attempt counts; they do not establish
significant geometry superiority, full chemical validity or correct mode
populations. Affine-context geometry assessment is a separate pending comparison.
The convex branch, its controls, screens and confirmations use 80,064 new oracle
queries and 320 xTB optimization attempts. The affine branch adds 37,184 oracle
queries. Source generation, inherited pretraining and earlier failed method
branches are additional costs.

\paragraph{Prespecified broader study: performance pending.}
An outcome-independent development manifest contains eight conditions with
9--20 atoms, including charged and open-shell cases and metal-containing
compositions. Every condition is retained. Source production targets 4,096
training and 512 development rows per condition. Complete energy replay, inverse,
Jacobian and checkpoint checks passed for all 24 condition/family smoke arms,
using 1,536 physical queries. These short checks have no performance standing.

The production design uses all three model families and two training streams
per condition, yielding 48 planned trained arms. Each arm receives 1,000 updates,
batch 16, and 17,024 physical queries: 16,000 training plus 512 base and 512 adapted
development evaluations. The planned adapter total is 817,152 queries; source
production has a separate planned budget of 36,864. One source-production attempt timed
out on a bulk RPC and was stopped, with an attempted-query bound of 0--8,192
and no qualified output. Its records are retained. The replacement bounds
requests and persists intermediate coordinates and scored chunks.
Complete broader performance results are not yet available. The reserved 722
conditions remain untouched.

\section{Limitations and remaining scientific gates}
The construction provides an exact relative-entropy accounting route, not an
absolute source-density estimator or a guarantee of molecular validity.
The base stays fixed; support and mode-coverage weaknesses can remain despite
local improvement. The element decomposition restricts homogeneous and
passive-context-poor systems. The energy target is a confined learned potential,
so results do not directly transfer to an experimental equilibrium ensemble.

External equivariant-flow and matched-sampling baselines are still required.
An augmented baseline's joint-KL upper bound must not be ranked as an exact
physical marginal KL. Physical-coordinate distribution diagnostics, credible
independent references, geometry/diversity checks and complete computation are
needed alongside any objective comparison. Strong broad gains, probability
calibration and method novelty remain open. This draft records the implemented
candidate and completed evidence while leaving those requirements explicit.
