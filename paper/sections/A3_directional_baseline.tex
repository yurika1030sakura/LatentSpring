\section{Frozen augmented-flow proposal comparison}
\label{app:eacf-directional}

The existing EACF refinement checkpoints transform a flow-matching source whose
density is unavailable. We therefore use their actual invertible augmented maps
as MH proposals, without assigning them an independent physical proposal density.
Given $x\in\HH$, draw $a\sim\rho(\cdot\mid x)=\mathcal N(x,0.1^2I_{3N})$
and a uniform direction $d\in\{0,1\}$. With $f_0=f$ and $f_1=f^{-1}$, compute
$(y,b)=f_d(x,a)$ and accept using
\begin{equation}
 \log R=-\frac{U(y)-U(x)}{kT}
       +\log\rho(b\mid y)-\log\rho(a\mid x)
       +\log\left|\det Df_d(x,a)\right|.
\end{equation}
Unsupported physical endpoints are rejected. On the extended state, the proposal
$(x,a,d)\mapsto(y,b,1-d)$ is an involution. The ratio above is the change in its
extended target density $\pi(x)\rho(a\mid x)/2$, including the volume change.
This is a standard augmented-space MH construction, not a new theoretical claim.
Auxiliary coordinates are unconstrained in $\mathbb R^{3N}$; projecting them onto
the physical COM subspace would change both their law and the Jacobian contract.

We check the pinned upstream forward and inverse interfaces on archived and fresh
auxiliaries, reflections and atom permutations. For one checkpoint per capacity,
the complete 57-dimensional Jacobian ($27$ physical and $30$ auxiliary dimensions)
agrees with the reported log volume to $2.92\times10^{-13}$ or better.
Every evaluated flow transformation, inverse, random draw, physical-query
coordinate and acceptance ratio is subsequently replayed. These checks qualify
the implementation; they do not certify finite-time equilibrium sampling.

Among supported EACF proposals, even the largest observed log acceptance ratio
is below $-10$ across the four runs. Both unfavorable potential changes and the
auxiliary/volume correction contribute. These checkpoints were trained as
refiners under their original objective, not to obtain high MH acceptance from
the warmed states. The result cannot establish failure of the architecture or
superiority over native EACF/FAB sampling. The physical site control is much
cheaper after preparation; longer physical controls also remain relevant.
Fresh-parent reuse and independent compositions are needed before promoting the
learned-vector pilot to a competitive sampling claim. No equilibrium distribution
or new chemical-discovery claim follows from these graph first hits.

\subsection{Completed fresh-parent reuse test}

A fresh geometry-only FM source uses 8,192 independent random draws with seeds
disjoint from the previous training and development streams. All source chunks,
input immutability and a repeated first batch are checked. Of 8,192 attempts,
72 pass the declared structural support (0.88\%), spanning four perceived
connectivities; 27 already have the diagnostic reference connectivity. No energy
ranking selects these parents, and no energy calls are used to generate or
screen this source. The low support rate remains a limitation of the initializer.
All 72 parents undergo the same 64-step physical preparation, with 9,360 raw
energy/force calls counting both orientations; the preparation and random stream replay.

Before evaluating the learned sampler, we fix a paired finite-cost comparison.
Each physical-site trajectory receives at most 1,024 raw calls, and each learned
trajectory at most 512, including initialization. Rejected supported proposals
still cost two calls; unsupported proposals remain recorded. A 1,024-microstep
cap retains any trajectory that cannot reach its query budget as censored.
For $N$ parents and learned inference budget $B$ per parent, let
$(q,r)=\operatorname{divmod}(9660/2,N)$. A measured physical control at budgets
$B+2q+2\mathbf 1[i<r]$ has exactly the same total raw-call cost as learning at $B$
plus its one-time 9,660-call training preparation. Comparisons outside the
recorded physical paths are unavailable, not extrapolated. Recorded generation,
fitting and sampling wall times are reported separately; the complete 8,192
source attempts remain explicit.

The prespecified primary comparison uses the entire eligible cohort at $B=512$,
with a paired mean restrained-potential difference and a parent-cluster bootstrap
after averaging the two algorithm replicas within each parent. Other budgets,
prefix sizes and diagnostic graph first hits are secondary. Initially-reference
parents are separated from later first hits. All four evaluations, their random
streams, per-parent ledgers and independent joint MH ratios now pass replay.
The complete round uses 230,544 new raw physical calls, including preparation;
the additional physical trajectory lengths support several cost comparisons.

At the primary matched total cost of 55,884 raw calls per method and replica,
the learned-minus-site mean restrained-potential difference is $-0.1735$\,eV,
with parent-bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.2595,-0.0994]$\,eV. The two replica
means are $-0.1944$ and $-0.1526$\,eV. Among the 45 parents not initially at the
diagnostic reference connectivity, the learned method reaches it for 44 and 45
parents, versus 30 and 33 for site. These are reference-graph first hits, not
discoveries of new molecules. The 128-call secondary matched-cost comparison
has an interval crossing zero; the evidence does not imply a win at every budget.
Figure~\ref{fig:fresh-reuse} retains the paired heterogeneity and source failures.

Stopping at a query count is
a finite-cost performance readout, not an equilibrium-preserving stopping rule;
this positive result does not establish a stationary Boltzmann ensemble or
independent-composition generalization. Source generation takes 956 seconds;
the full site paths take about 387 seconds per replica and the shorter learned
paths about 240 seconds. These unequal-length timings are reported separately
and are not a matched-wall-time speedup claim.

\subsection{Independent approximate-energy check}

We evaluate all 288 primary endpoints and all 72 common starts with GFN2-xTB~\citep{gfn2xtb}
at fixed coordinates, preserving total charge and spin. Four additional inverted
starts check the numerical symmetry contract. All 364 single-point/gradient
calculations converge and all raw logs and input coordinates are reparsed.
The learned-minus-site mean GFN2 energy difference is $-0.4132$\,eV, with
parent-bootstrap 95\% interval $[-0.5918,-0.2462]$\,eV; both replicas favor the
learned endpoints. Adding the same restraint changes this mean by less than
$2\times10^{-5}$\,eV. This corroborates the energetic ordering with a second
approximate potential, rather than establishing DFT accuracy, chemical stability
or a Boltzmann ensemble.

\subsection{Frozen six-composition transfer result}

We select six additional development compositions by a fixed hash, with three
each in the 8--12 and 13--24 atom size strata. Selection uses composition,
neutral singlet electronic state and H/halogen eligibility, with no energy or
generated-geometry ranking. The frozen source produces 512 draws per composition;
all source failures remain in the denominator. We evaluate the first 16 supported
parents per composition, using the original learned checkpoints without fitting
to these coordinates. Training cost is charged once across all 96 parents.

\subsection{Completed stronger physical-width controls}
\label{app:sharp-controls}

We freeze physical-site concentrations 64 and 400 on both the original 72 parents
and the 96 transfer parents, with two algorithm replicas. All other proposal,
target and local-update settings are unchanged. Concentration 64 uses the earlier
model's pre-existing parameter bound as a control setting; concentration 400 is a
rounded scale suggested by training-only angular-curvature probes. We report
both zero-incremental calibration reuse and a fully charged 10,108-call
calibration scenario for 400 (9,660 inherited preparation calls plus 448 probe
calls). The 28 physical-control arms use 688,128 new raw calls; every trajectory,
random stream, per-parent ledger and joint MH ratio is audited. No endpoints are
missing from these comparisons.
