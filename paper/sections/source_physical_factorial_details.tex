\subsection{Matched experiment on sources and physical adaptation}\label{sec:factorial-details}
The Gaussian cell follows the same native-source teacher construction as the
harmonic cell. For each continuation, the frozen Gaussian model generates 32
anchors for each of the same eight training compositions. All 256 attempts are
retained in the record; invalid anchors are excluded without replenishment.
The eight-particle force-escort recipe, temperature, perturbation bounds, source
seeds and 1,000-step training schedule are unchanged. Each physical student has
its own replay student initialized from the same Gaussian base. Their parameter
difference is added to that base with coefficient one. The reference examples,
source randomness, training times and teacher-selection draws are paired within
each physical/replay comparison.

The two Gaussian continuations retain 160 and 138 anchors after the common
candidate-support checks. Teacher preparation uses 5,276 raw eSEN queries in
total, including the original recipe's stored complete-work diagnostics; the
students use uniform weights over supported force-shifted candidates. This
preserves the original maximum preparation budget while allowing actual
source-specific support and query counts to differ. Four new 1,000-step student
fits produce the two paired Gaussian updates. The harmonic physical cell and
both unadapted cells reuse their previously audited checkpoints and outputs.

The new Gaussian physical models generate 1,536 unoptimized evaluation outputs
on the existing 24-composition panel at 128 network calls per attempt. Their
eSEN readout adds 3,072 queries, and GFN2 evaluation adds 1,632 attempts including
reference inversion checks. One GFN2 calculation fails, on a graph-invalid
output; every graph-valid output and every reference check succeeds. The audit
reconstructs teacher proposals and graph restrictions, replays the uniform
candidate draws and parameter arithmetic, and reparses the raw structural and
physical evaluations. This is a fixed follow-up on an already evaluated panel.

For Gaussian, the physical-minus-base energy change among paired, jointly valid
outputs is $-0.01596$ eV/atom under eSEN (95\% interval [-0.02182,-0.01021]) and
$-0.01465$ under GFN2 ([-0.02041,-0.00929]). Force-RMS changes are
$-0.3795$ eV/\AA\ ([-0.4618,-0.3045]) and $-0.4618$
([-0.6195,-0.3370]), respectively. These results show that replay-subtracted
physical learning also benefits the Gaussian source.

At force RMS at most 5 eV/\AA, the GFN2 physical-update gains in continuations
1 and 2 are 4.56 and 5.34 percentage points for Gaussian, and 3.13 and 2.99 for
harmonic. The harmonic-minus-Gaussian contrast after physical training is
16.28 and $-2.34$ points across the two continuations, although its pooled
difference is positive. Thus pooled source improvement and training-repeat
variability are both visible. These results support separate benefits from the
source and the update; they do not imply a positive statistical interaction.
