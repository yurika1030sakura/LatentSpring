\subsection{Matched experiment on sources and physical adaptation}\label{sec:factorial-details}
We complete the four-setting comparison by constructing force targets from Gaussian-source outputs. Each run generates 32 anchors on each of the same eight training compositions, records all 256 attempts, and discards invalid anchors without replacement. The eight-candidate escort, temperature, perturbation bounds, source seeds, and 1,000-update schedule match the harmonic procedure. Physical and replay students share the Gaussian base, references, source randomness, times, and teacher draws; their parameter difference is added with coefficient one.

After support checks, the Gaussian runs retain 160 and 138 anchors. Preparation uses 5,276 eSEN queries, including complete-work diagnostics; the students sample uniformly from admissible shifted candidates. Maximum preparation budgets match, while realized anchor and query counts vary by source. Four new 1,000-update student fits yield two adapted Gaussian models. Harmonic and unadapted results reuse the existing checkpoints and outputs.

The new models generate 1,536 raw outputs on the existing 24-composition panel at 128 calls each. Evaluation adds 3,072 eSEN queries and 1,632 GFN2 attempts including reference inversions. One graph-invalid calculation fails; all graph-valid and reference calculations succeed. Checks reconstruct proposals and graph restrictions, replay target draws and parameter arithmetic, and reparse scores. The comparison is a follow-up on the previously evaluated panel.

For Gaussian, adaptation changes jointly valid paired energy by $-0.01596$ eV/atom under eSEN (95\% interval [-0.02182,-0.01021]) and $-0.01465$ under GFN2 ([-0.02041,-0.00929]). Force-RMS changes are $-0.3795$ eV/\AA\ ([-0.4618,-0.3045]) and $-0.4618$ ([-0.6195,-0.3370]).

At 5 eV/\AA, GFN2 joint-yield gains are 4.56 and 5.34 points for Gaussian and 3.13 and 2.99 for harmonic. After adaptation, harmonic minus Gaussian is 16.28 in run 1 and $-2.34$ in run 2. These results show within-source adaptation benefits and a positive pooled source difference, with substantial run variation; a positive source-by-update interaction is not established.
