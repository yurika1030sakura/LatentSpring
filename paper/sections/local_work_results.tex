On jointly valid pairs, complete-work training lowers energy relative to uniform escort by 0.00758 eV/atom (signed-change 95\% interval $[-0.01424,-0.00113]$) and force RMS by 0.16174 eV/\AA\ ($[-0.27660,-0.05590]$). Both runs and the all-output comparison favor complete work.

Nevertheless, all three direct students underperform the frozen parent. Complete work gives 55.16\% graph validity versus 62.11\%, and raises jointly valid paired energy by 0.10587 eV/atom ($[0.09009,0.12168]$). Replay also degrades performance, motivating subtraction of its matched update.

Teacher preparation uses 6,232 queries: 702 at anchors and 5,530 at allowed candidates. Uniform escort can omit candidate energies; replay requires no physical queries. Evaluation of the four models adds 10,240 queries, separate from sampling.
