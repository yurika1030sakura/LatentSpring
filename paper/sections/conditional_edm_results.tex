\subsection{An independently adapted diffusion baseline}\label{sec:edm}
We additionally adapt the official EGNN/EDM backbone \citep{edm} to the same
composition-conditioned task. Positions follow its variance-preserving diffusion
schedule, while clean atom categories are conditions during both training and
sampling. The GEOM-pretrained checkpoint receives 30,000 updates on the same
3,000 OMol25 training structures used by each source continuation, with its original
$10^{-4}$ learning rate. We evaluate both 128 network evaluations and the full 1,001-call
sampler, including the final observation step, on all 24 fresh compositions.

This comparison tests adaptation to the same task and training data with a
different pretrained architecture and objective. EDM has 2.38 million parameters
and one denoiser pass per update; LatentSpring has 5.90 million and two. The longer
EDM continuation takes 481.51/463.61 seconds, compared with approximately
570--590 seconds for the source-model continuations. Its 128-call sampler takes
48.54/47.94 seconds per 768 outputs, and its 1,001-call sampler takes
377.65/374.12 seconds. These timings distinguish call count from computational
cost. Pretraining histories differ, and composition-overlap checks cover the
processed OMol25 corpora. The matched Gaussian--harmonic experiments provide the
controlled comparison of source choice.
