\subsection{An independently adapted diffusion baseline}\label{sec:edm}
We additionally adapt the official EGNN/EDM backbone \citep{edm} to the same
composition-conditioned task. Positions follow its variance-preserving diffusion
schedule, while clean atom categories are conditions during both training and
sampling. The GEOM-pretrained checkpoint receives 30,000 updates on the same
3,000 OMol25 FIT rows used by each source continuation, with its original
$10^{-4}$ learning rate. We evaluate both 128 denoiser calls and the full 1,001-call
sampler, including the final observation step, on all 24 fresh compositions.

This complements the matched source ablations by testing a different pretrained
backbone and generative objective. The backbones and pretraining histories differ:
EDM has 2.38 million parameters and one denoiser pass per update, versus 5.90 million
and two for the source-SC models. Its longer continuation strengthens task adaptation,
but does not match total pretraining resources or certify separation from its GEOM
pretraining data. The table reports this conditional adaptation protocol, not
EDM's published joint-generation benchmark. Equal call counts also differ in
FLOPs; generation and training times are recorded separately.
