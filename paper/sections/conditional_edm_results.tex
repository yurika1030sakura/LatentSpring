\subsection{An independently adapted diffusion baseline}\label{sec:edm}
We also adapt the official EGNN/EDM backbone \citep{edm} to composition-conditioned
coordinate generation. Atom categories are fixed conditions during training and
sampling, while positions follow EDM's variance-preserving diffusion schedule.
Starting from its GEOM-pretrained checkpoint, we train for 30,000 updates on the
same 3,000 OMol25 structures used in each source-model run, retaining the original
learning rate of $10^{-4}$. We evaluate all 24 new compositions with both a
128-call sampler and the full 1,001-call sampler, including the final observation
step.

This baseline uses the same task and adaptation data but a different pretrained
architecture and objective. EDM has 2.38 million parameters and one denoiser pass
per update; LatentSpring has 5.90 million parameters and two passes. The longer
EDM fine-tuning runs take 481.51 and 463.61 seconds, compared with about 570--590
seconds for the source-model runs. For 768 outputs, EDM's 128-call sampler takes
48.54 and 47.94 seconds; its 1,001-call sampler takes 377.65 and 374.12 seconds.
Matching network-call counts is therefore not the same as matching computational
cost. Pretraining histories also differ, and the composition-overlap checks cover
only the processed OMol25 corpora. We use the matched Gaussian--harmonic
experiments, rather than this baseline, to isolate the effect of source choice.
