\section{Earlier source-study setup}\label{app:source-study-setup}
\paragraph{Task and training.}
The earlier source experiments adapt a pretrained molecular flow to neutral singlet organic
compositions. Every composition contains C and H, with elements drawn from
H/B/C/N/O/F/Si/P/S/Cl/Br/I. Training covers 8--40 atoms and evaluation extends
to 64; the implementation supports up to 200 atoms. The initial two fine-tuning
runs start from the same pretrained checkpoint. Each uses 3,000 distinct
OMol25 structures and 3,000 optimizer updates. Within a run, source variants share
the training data and initial weights. The network has 5,904,369 parameters.
Learning rates are $2\cdot10^{-5}$ for the backbone and $3\cdot10^{-4}$ for
self-conditioning modules. Three additional source runs repeat these two data
blocks with new random seeds and report all 1,000-, 3,000-, and 6,000-update checkpoints.

All source variants use the same filtered training subset. In addition to the
chemical-graph checks, selection requires a spanning scaffold with bounded edge
lengths and atom degrees. This scaffold is a data-selection filter, not an input
to the model. Appendix~\ref{app:data-evaluation} specifies it.

\paragraph{Evaluation sets.}
The source study uses four composition sets: 12 for development, 10 for an additional test, 24
for a further test, and 64 for a broader size comparison. Exact atom-count checks
confirm that the latter three sets are absent from both processed OMol25 corpora.
Their reference coordinates are used only to determine eligibility, not for
training. Initial comparisons reuse two fine-tuned models, and the wider source
study adds three training repetitions. Shared-EGNN experiments train from scratch;
the final correction study includes five paired initializations. Physical studies
compare models on common compositions and identify the panel alongside each result.
Selection and source-control details are in Appendix~\ref{app:source-panels}.

\paragraph{Metrics and uncertainty.}
The 12- and 10-composition sets use 64 samples per composition, and the
24-composition set uses 32. Geometric validity requires a connected contact graph
and no severe atom overlap, with distance thresholds of 1.25 and 0.6 times the
summed covalent radii, respectively. Chemical-graph validity additionally requires
RDKit to infer a connected, sanitized bond graph with the requested total charge.
We divide valid outputs by \emph{all attempts}, including failures and validator
exceptions. Graph diversity counts distinct inferred connectivities within each
composition. Timings include the full generation procedure.
Appendix~\ref{app:data-evaluation} gives the detailed checks.

Unless stated otherwise, 95\% confidence intervals come from paired bootstrap
resampling of generation draws, with the trained models and compositions held
fixed. We also resample compositions to describe variation across the evaluation
set. Physical scores are computed on saved raw coordinates, using the stated
charge and spin and approximate energy models.
