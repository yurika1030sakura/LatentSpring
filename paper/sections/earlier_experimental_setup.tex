\section{Earlier source-study setup}\label{app:source-study-setup}
\paragraph{Task and training.}
The source studies adapt a pretrained flow to neutral organic singlets containing C and H, with elements from H/B/C/N/O/F/Si/P/S/Cl/Br/I. Training covers 8--40 atoms and evaluation extends to 64, within the implementation limit of 200. The first two runs share a pretrained checkpoint. Each uses 3,000 distinct OMol25 structures and 3,000 updates, with identical data and initial weights across source variants. The network has 5,904,369 parameters; learning rates are $2\cdot10^{-5}$ for the backbone and $3\cdot10^{-4}$ for self-conditioning modules. Three further runs reuse the two data blocks with new seeds and evaluate all 1,000-, 3,000-, and 6,000-update checkpoints.

All sources use the same training subset, screened for chemical-graph validity and a spanning scaffold with bounded lengths and degrees. The scaffold is a training-data filter (Appendix~\ref{app:data-evaluation}), not a model input.

\paragraph{FlowMol sampler.}
The coordinate-shaped output $D_\theta$ defines velocity through the centered increment $D_\theta-X_t$. In the original midpoint implementation, both field evaluations use the interval midpoint time: the first at the current coordinates and the second at the half-step coordinates. Source-only comparisons add centered Gaussian terminal noise of standard deviation 0.025\,\AA; learned-correction sampling omits it. These settings apply to the earlier FlowMol studies rather than the main shared-EGNN sampler.

\paragraph{Evaluation panels.}
The source study uses 12 development compositions, 10 additional-test compositions, a further 24-composition test, and a 64-composition size panel. Exact atom-count checks exclude the latter three panels from both processed OMol25 corpora. Their reference coordinates determine eligibility only. Initial studies use two fine-tuning runs, and the wider source study adds three repetitions. The shared-EGNN models train from scratch; the final correction study uses five paired initializations. Each physical comparison identifies its panel. Appendix~\ref{app:source-panels} gives selection and source-control details.

\paragraph{Evaluation and uncertainty.}
The 12- and 10-composition panels use 64 draws per composition; the 24-composition panel uses 32. Contact connectivity and overlap thresholds are 1.25 and 0.6 times the summed covalent radii. Chemical-graph validity additionally requires a connected, sanitized RDKit graph with the requested charge. Rates count all attempts, including failures and validator exceptions. Diversity counts distinct inferred connectivities within composition, and timings cover the full sampling procedure (Appendix~\ref{app:data-evaluation}).

Unless specified otherwise, paired 95\% bootstrap intervals resample generation draws conditional on the fitted models and compositions. Additional composition-bootstrap intervals describe variation across the evaluation panel. Physical scores use saved raw coordinates, the stated charge and spin, and the specified approximate potential.
