\paragraph{Composition sets.}
Checking reference structures leaves 31 eligible compositions out of 78 that pass
the metadata conditions. Of these, 21 had appeared in earlier development
experiments. We use a fixed subset of 12 for source development and all 10
remaining compositions for a subsequent test. These 10 have 17--28 atoms and
are absent from 3,902,107 processed training rows and 39,415 validation rows,
verified by exact atomic-count vectors. Reference geometries only determine
eligibility. Both sets use the same two fine-tuning runs; the further
24-composition test is described in Section~\ref{sec:fresh-physics}.

On the development set, the harmonic source improves graph validity over the
isotropic Gaussian by 5.86 percentage points (paired 95\% CI [2.67,8.98];
composition-bootstrap interval [0.52,12.17]). Both runs improve, with a larger
gain in run 1. Harmonic also exceeds the narrow shell-tree source by 4.23 points;
the shell-minus-harmonic interval is [-7.29,-1.11].

On the 10-composition test, harmonic improves graph validity from 52.73\% to
61.56\%, a gain of 8.83 points (paired 95\% CI [5.08,12.50]; composition-bootstrap
interval [4.84,12.66]). Geometric validity improves by 9.69 points [6.33,12.89].
Every graph-valid output has distinct inferred connectivity within its composition,
and no validator exceptions occur. Generation time is nearly unchanged:
harmonic/Gaussian take 175.73/175.43 seconds for 640 samples in run 1 and
178.20/177.74 seconds in run 2.
