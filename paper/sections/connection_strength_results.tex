\subsection{Correction strength and the quality tradeoff}\label{app:connection-strength}
The validation study above selects the largest allowed strength for both
generators. We extend the common grid to $\{0,1/4,1/2,1,2,4\}$, reusing the
same eight validation compositions and random seeds. Only strengths two and
four require new samples. Parents, heads, force labels, and fitting budgets
are unchanged. The applied correction bound scales with the strength $\eta$;
the bound stated for $\eta=1$ is not retained at larger strengths.

We compare two validation choices. A conservative rule permits at most a
one-point decrease in pooled graph validity and a three-point decrease in each
fit, relative to strength one. It retains strength one for both generators.
Selecting solely by joint yield chooses strength four for both, with validation
joint yields of 31.64\% for FM and 28.91\% for GAGA. This second objective is
declared after the conservative screen, before any new test generation. Both
choices are then evaluated on the same 32 previously ungenerated compositions,
16 each with 17--28 and 29--40 atoms, with 16 outputs per composition and fit.

Stronger correction improves FM joint yield by 5.66 percentage points
(95\% composition-bootstrap interval $[2.73,9.38]$), with gains of 7.23 and
4.10 points in the two fits. The GAGA change is $+3.52$ points ($[0.00,7.52]$).
At strength four, FM minus GAGA is $+0.78$ points in joint yield
($[-4.00,6.54]$) and $+1.17$ points in graph validity ($[-2.93,5.86]$).
The fit-specific joint differences are $-1.37$ and $+2.93$ points, so the
pooled ordering is not stable across fitted models. The recorded FM sampling
time is 14.6\% lower, with 64 head calls per output versus GAGA's 128;
both use 128 backbone calls. These are measured sampling times, not equal
training cost or a hardware-independent speed guarantee.

Across all outputs, stronger correction lowers FM's mean GFN2 energy by
0.0622 eV/atom, with signed-change interval $[-0.0769,-0.0479]$.
At strength four, FM's mean energy remains 0.0527 eV/atom above GAGA's
($[0.0273,0.0786]$). These composition-paired readouts include invalid outputs
and different inferred connectivities; they are not equilibrium free-energy
differences. All graph-valid outputs within each 16-draw composition/fit cell
have distinct canonical connectivities when stereochemistry is omitted.

The remaining errors are predominantly structural. At strength four, 533 of
1,024 FM outputs and 579 GAGA outputs fail the geometric connectedness test;
only two FM outputs and no GAGA outputs overlap at the prescribed radius
threshold, with both overlaps among the disconnected cases. Of the graph-valid
outputs, 213/227 for FM and 205/215 for GAGA pass the force threshold.
The extension adds 1,024 validation outputs and 4,096 confirmation outputs,
each receiving a GFN2 attempt, with no new fitting or eSEN calls. One GAGA
strength-one GFN2 calculation fails and remains in the denominator; the other
4,095 confirmation calculations succeed.
