\paragraph{Control for source covariance.}
To test the role of second moments, we construct a Gaussian from a 128-tree
estimate of the harmonic mixture covariance and symmetrize it over identical
species. Calibration against an independent 8,192-tree estimate gives
1.85--6.25\% relative Frobenius error across the 22 compositions. This control
uses the same data, optimizer, network and sampling budgets. It is evaluated
after the initial source comparison against the frozen harmonic checkpoints.
