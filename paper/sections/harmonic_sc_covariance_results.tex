\paragraph{Covariance-matched control.}
We approximate the harmonic mixture covariance with 128 sampled trees and average over permutations of identical elements. Relative Frobenius error against an independent 8,192-tree estimate is 1.85--6.25\% across the 22 compositions. New single-Gaussian models use this covariance with matched data, optimization, architecture, and sampling budgets, and are compared with the frozen harmonic models.
