\section{Constrained geodesic reconstruction: development evidence}
\label{app:geodesic-reconstruction}

The failure against stronger physical controls motivates a reconstruction of the
geometric proposal. This appendix records an independently checked physical
proposal and its training-panel diagnostics. No new neural model is fitted in
this round, and these results do not establish a learned advantage.
Geodesic sampling on the sphere has established precedents, including
\citet{habeck2025geodesic}; their convergence guarantees do not transfer to this
molecular kernel without a separate argument.

\paragraph{Feasible directions and implemented probability law.}
Fix a terminal root radius $r$, its anchor and all passive positions. Relative
to the anchor, a passive vector $w$ with a required minimum root distance $d$
gives the spherical half-space
\begin{equation}
 u^\top w\leq\frac{r^2+\|w\|^2-d^2}{2r},\qquad u\in\sphere.
\end{equation}
We use the declared graph builder's contact thresholds, a small numerical margin,
and separate root--anchor and passive-geometry checks. The final desired graph
and sanitizer are still checked. These are algorithmic support constraints,
not quantum stability certificates.
For a reference direction $u_0$ and a uniform unit tangent $v$, write
$u(\theta)=\cos(\theta)u_0+\sin(\theta)v$.
Each half-space becomes a scalar trigonometric inequality; its exact interval
intersection gives the feasible arcs on this circle. Empty intervals are failed
proposals without repeated sampling until success.

Segments of width at most $\pi/64$ linearly interpolate endpoint log scores.
For endpoint difference $\delta=g_1-g_0$ and segment width $h$, the mass is
$h\exp(g_0)\operatorname{exprel}(\delta)$, where
$\operatorname{exprel}(\delta)=(\exp(\delta)-1)/\delta$ with its continuous limit
at zero. Segment selection and inverse-CDF sampling use this same normalized
interpolant. For $u\ne\pm u_0$, let $\theta\in(0,\pi)$ be its angular separation
and let $v$ point toward $u$ in the tangent plane. The density per sphere area is
\begin{equation}
 q(u\mid u_0,C)=
 \frac{p_v(\theta\mid C)+p_{-v}(2\pi-\theta\mid C)}
 {2\pi\lvert\sin\theta\rvert}.
\end{equation}
Both circle preimages and their actual normalizers are included. Failure mass
remains a self transition; it is not divided out by an unknown success rate.

\paragraph{Complete joint density.}
Both lognormal radii are sampled before the two root directions. Each direction
uses the actual partial context, and unsupported passive arrangements fail once.
The coordinate density includes both angular factors and the Cartesian radial
factors $r_1^{-3}r_2^{-3}$. The COM chart has a constant factor
$((N-2)/N)^{3/2}$ which cancels for this same-size reverse move. We sample one
fair decoder order but evaluate the candidate density as
$q_{\mathrm{joint}}=(q_{01}+q_{10})/2$, in both directions. This is ordinary
mixture marginalization. The complete MH ratio also includes the forward/reverse
eligible-action counts. Zero reverse density gives rejection before an energy
query. Tests check the full six-coordinate chart volume, symmetries, and one
complete joint MH update started from independent exact samples of a known
joint target, in addition to the root-only checks.

\paragraph{Support repair and a failed local teacher.}
The audited training source has support counts $45,32,106,99,0,19,0,96$ out of
256 attempts per composition. All zeros are retained. The six nonempty cases
supply 96 prepared parents. Fixed-context force/work probes yield 438 local
surrogates, but 47 have a negative axial coefficient: their unrestricted
full-sphere teacher produces only 16 supported draws out of 376 attempts.
Restricting to feasible arcs raises this to 376/376; all 14,016 root arc draws
in the four-score screen preserve the graph and positive reverse density.
In the complete joint support screen, each arc score gives 745/768 supported
positive-reverse proposals versus 690/768 for legacy site64. All 3,072 attempts
replay; 750 independent forward/reverse density checks have maximum log error
$4.27\times10^{-14}$.

Support does not ensure useful movement. On 206 fixed FIT contexts, 824 frozen
first-draw endpoints cost 1,648 paired raw oracle calls. The fitted score has
expected MH acceptance 0.725 but expected potential change only $-0.0453$\,eV;
uniform arcs give 0.248 and $-0.1440$\,eV. These parent-then-composition means are
root-only training diagnostics with the per-context score held fixed under
reversal. Local fit or high acceptance cannot serve as a sufficient learning
gate, and unrestricted teacher-density distillation is not justified.

\paragraph{Complete-chain physical pilot.}
A frozen protocol compares legacy site64, uniform arcs and site arcs on all 48
FIT parents from four compositions, with two algorithm seeds. Each parent has
128 raw calls including initialization; the shared local and force-rotation
moves are unchanged. All 24 arms reach the budget, using 36,864 raw calls in
total. Full trajectory replay and 4,668 independent joint MH checks pass.
Table~\ref{tab:arc-training-chain} reports potential changes at the largest
frozen readout. Algorithm seeds are averaged within parent before composition
averaging. Site arcs also raise the mean number of visited constitutional
connectivities from 1.458 to 1.719, but aggregate measured sampling time rises
from 98.87 to 121.04 seconds. There is no matched-wall-time superiority claim.
Uniform arcs' root-only advantage does not establish a complete-chain benefit.

Site arcs minus legacy site64 give $-0.07615$\,eV, with descriptive parent-bootstrap

site arcs, with condition 1 contributing much of the mean. Uniform arcs minus

The 32-call site-arc interval crosses zero. These exploratory intervals are
not adjusted for the multiple methods and readouts. The physical construction
provides a stronger next baseline; it does not establish AI novelty, equilibrium
sampling, or held-out superiority. Full outcomes and query ledgers are retained
in the versioned joint-arc budget summary and its frozen protocol.

Finally, a necessary degree bound identifies empty connected support for
training condition 6, composition C$_4$H$_{10}$OFCl, under the pinned graph
builder: maximum degree sum 31 is smaller than the 32 required for a connected
17-atom graph. Its 256 source failures remain in the denominator. This is an
incompatibility with this algorithmic target, not a statement that the composition
is chemically impossible. The other zero-support case remains unresolved.

\subsection{Conditional nonlocal energy learning}
\label{app:nonlocal-arc-learning}

We next replace the one-vector surrogate by a conditional scalar energy. Masked
message passing uses passive geometry, the desired graph, root radius, atomic
features and the original electronic condition. It predicts bounded coefficients
of radial basis functions of candidate root-to-passive distances. Their sum,
with a smooth distance cutoff, gives a residual energy in eV. The physical site
score converted to energy and the analytic COM confinement term are added.
The root direction is absent from all context features; only the candidate
readout receives it. Tests check O(3), translation and atom-reordering invariance.
This is a conditional neural energy construction, not a new physical law.
Learning an effective model to accelerate corrected Monte Carlo has established
precedents~\citep{liu2017slmc,shen2018slmc}.

Within each fixed context we fit potential differences relative to its center.
A second objective adds the tangential derivative of that same scalar energy,
using the supplied physical forces with the COM correction. Both errors use
Huber losses at a 0.1\,eV scale; the derivative term has weight 0.1. At inference,
the score is minus the surrogate energy divided by $kT$. Every interpolation
endpoint uses this actual scalar score. The complete joint decoder recomputes
all partial contexts in both orders and both directions; independent NumPy
readout and interval quadrature check the resulting densities. The scalar model
is never reinterpreted as a one-vector vMF proposal.

The fixed dataset contains 438 contexts: 206 FIT, 76 from withheld parents and
156 from withheld compositions. Every context includes the center, all supported
local probes and four already frozen feasible-arc endpoints. The two local
check directions on FIT parents are also excluded from training. The additional
928 withheld endpoints cost 1,856 raw calls and have full independent oracle
replay. None of the 722 reserved final outcomes is queried.

Two objectives and two seeds each run 800 fixed optimizer steps with the same
parent-balanced minibatch streams. We evaluate the final checkpoint without
selecting a favorable intermediate checkpoint. The model has 5,689 parameters.
Table~\ref{tab:nonlocal-prediction} reports nonlocal work prediction after
averaging contexts within parent, parents within composition, then compositions.
All four models improve on the physical score. The added force loss has no
established decisive advantage over work-only training. An independent audit
rebuilds all data and split masks, re-evaluates every final context metric and
both physical baselines, and checks finite differences of the actual trained
loss (maximum gradient error $1.19\times10^{-8}$). The complete optimizer
trajectory has not been independently replayed.
