\section{Conditional action and geometry ablations}
\label{app:action-geometry}

The preceding geometric utility model leaves chemical action selection uniform.
We next test a bounded conditional action policy together with the placement
model. The local/rotation/exchange schedule remains fixed. An invariant graph
encoder uses continuous atomic descriptors, pair distances, perceived bonds and
the original electronic condition. Action features are symmetrized under swapping
the two exchanging leaves and their anchors. A bounded logit is normalized over
all eligible exchanges; no move-family head is learned. Empty legal sets give
self transitions. This reuses the earlier action encoder but changes its
coordinate proposal and training objective; it does not erase the earlier
selector's failure.

For augmented action/coordinate densities, write
$Q_{\theta,f}=p_\theta(a\mid x)q_\theta(y\mid x,a)$ and use the newly enumerated
inverse action set at $y$ for $Q_{\theta,r}$. The behavioral forward density is
$Q_{b,f}=q_b(y\mid x,a)/N_f$. The observed utility is
\begin{equation}
 r(x,y)\min\!\left\{\frac{Q_{\theta,f}}{Q_{b,f}},
 e^{-\Delta U/kT}\frac{Q_{\theta,r}}{Q_{b,f}}\right\}.
\end{equation}
The uniform action-count ratio must not be added again. Bounded action logits
$B_a$ and root log-scores $B_g$ imply a total proposal-ratio bound
$\exp(2B_a+4B_g)$ on common support. The fixed comparisons use action-only
$B_a=1$, geometry-only $B_g=0.5$, and joint $B_a=0.5,B_g=0.25$, each with total
bound $e^2$. All start at the same physical proposal and use the same data,
optimizer, minibatch draws and 300 steps with two seeds. Active parameter counts
and runtime are recorded separately; they are not claimed matched.

The six arms retain the same 36 fitting and 12 internal-selection parents,
1,671 attempts and 18,110 data-construction calls. No new oracle calls are used.
Every final/baseline metric is replayed; 576 full forward/reverse densities are
checked with independent action enumeration, NumPy action-network evaluation
and arc quadrature. Maximum density disagreement is $4.98\times10^{-14}$.
Eight trained-head objective finite differences have maximum error
$3.17\times10^{-10}$. Geometry-only parameters reproduce the preceding models
to within $7.8\times10^{-16}$.

The joint model does not establish improvement over physics. Against geometry
alone, its differences are $-9.50\times10^{-5}$ and $-9.35\times10^{-5}$\,eV per
call, with intervals $[-22.23,1.12]\times10^{-5}$ and
$[-19.80,-0.30]\times10^{-5}$. Joint learning improves on action-only learning
in this empirical proxy, but that comparison does not establish a useful
combined method. The fixed bound allocation limits the scope of the conclusion;
it does not rule out every possible joint policy. These weights do not justify
molecular scale-up. The earlier geometry-only fresh-proposal null result and
scalar-model chain failure remain unchanged. No independent final-test or
equilibrium advantage is established.
