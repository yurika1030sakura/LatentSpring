\section{Physical quality of the matched generators}\label{app:generator-quality}
We extend the independent EGNN comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:matched-generators} with physical readouts of its 16,384 saved
128-call outputs. The models, geometries, composition panel and generation
budgets are unchanged. The readout protocol is fixed before any of these
physical scores are evaluated. eSEN scores every output and its reflection;
GFN2 scores every graph-valid output and both reference parities. All 2,198
GFN2 calculations succeed, including 256 reference checks, whose inversion
tests pass. The eSEN readout uses 33,024 evaluations.

A quality yield is the fraction of \emph{all attempted outputs} that pass the
chemical-graph assay and a specified physical threshold. Invalid structures
therefore remain in the denominator. Figure~\ref{fig:generator-quality} varies
the force-RMS threshold rather than assigning a single cutoff to physical
validity. These thresholds describe the readout and do not certify a stationary
geometry. The methods share the training budget and architecture specified in
Appendix~\ref{app:matched-generators}; no physical update is added to these
source and generator-objective controls.

The source's gain in graph validity does not imply lower physical scores among
the resulting valid structures. GAGA also has a stronger aggregate physical
readout in this matched setting. The physical-learning experiments separately
test whether local physical targets improve a fixed harmonic generator.
