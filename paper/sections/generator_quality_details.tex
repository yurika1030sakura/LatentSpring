\section{Physical quality of the matched generators}\label{app:generator-quality}
We evaluate physical scores for the 16,384 saved outputs from the independent
EGNN comparison in Section~\ref{sec:matched-generators}. These are the original
128-call outputs; no models are retrained and no coordinates are changed. We fix
the physical-evaluation protocol before computing these scores. eSEN evaluates
every output and its reflection. GFN2 evaluates every graph-valid output and
both inversions of each reference. All 2,198 GFN2 calculations succeed, including
256 reference checks that pass inversion tests. eSEN uses 33,024 evaluations.

Joint quality yield divides outputs that pass both graph validity and a physical
threshold by \emph{all generation attempts}. Graph-invalid structures therefore
remain in the denominator. Figure~\ref{fig:generator-quality} sweeps the
force-RMS threshold instead of treating one cutoff as a universal definition of
physical validity. Passing a threshold does not certify a stationary geometry.
These models share the architecture and training budget in
Appendix~\ref{app:matched-generators}; none receives a physical update in this
source/objective comparison.

Higher graph validity from the harmonic source does not, by itself, imply better
physical scores among valid outputs. GAGA has stronger pooled physical results
in this matched setting. The separate physical-adaptation experiments test the
effect of local physical targets on trained generators.
