\section{Separate capped-relaxation diagnostic}\label{app:relaxation}
To measure relaxation burden directly, select draw indices 0--3 for each of the
24 fresh compositions, both existing continuations, and the frozen harmonic and
force-update variants: 384 generated structures. Include one original data
reference per composition, giving 408 trajectories. Selection uses no energy or
graph filtering. ASE BFGS optimizes copies with a maximum step of 0.1\,\AA\ and
maximum-atom-force convergence threshold of 0.05 eV/\AA, recording steps 0, 5 and 20.
Initial single-point values are reused; subsequent steps use the same GFN2-xTB
potential and original electronic conditions. These optimized copies never replace
raw generation or enter training. The diagnostic uses 8,142 new single-point
attempts, plus one separately recorded integration-smoke query.

No generated trajectory reaches the force threshold at either cap; one reference
does at 20 steps. One force-update trajectory has an SCC failure at step 7 and
remains in the denominator as unavailable at step 20. RMSD summaries condition on
available trajectories. The force-update minus harmonic graph-rate difference is
zero at both caps, with conditional paired 95\% interval $[-4.17,4.17]$ percentage
points. These results do not establish reduced relaxation burden. The low reference
convergence rate also shows that the original data coordinates cannot be assumed
to be GFN2 minima. Force convergence alone would not certify a positive Hessian or
a stable minimum. This diagnostic makes no equilibrium-distribution claim.
