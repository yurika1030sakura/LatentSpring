\section{Geometric recovery training and correction}\label{app:geometric-recovery}
\paragraph{Backbone continuation.}
Starting from each initial FM fit, we run 4,000 further AdamW updates with batch
size 32, learning rate $10^{-5}$, gradient-norm limit one, and EMA decay capped
at 0.999. Odd updates use the original two-pass FM loss. Even updates sample
progress uniformly from $[0.60,0.95]$ and regress both provisional endpoints to
the paired OMol25 reference. The continuation control uses these same updates,
batches and progress values without extra perturbations or local geometry terms.

Recovery training adds centered perturbations to the late interpolant. Each
atom is independently selected with probability 0.25 for Gaussian noise, whose
standard deviation is chosen uniformly from 0.05, 0.15 and 0.30\,\AA\ per molecule.
With probability 0.5, a random spatial half of the molecule receives a shared
Gaussian displacement with standard deviation 0.35\,\AA. Centering removes the
mean displacement. The reference coordinates remain the regression target.

The even-update objective is four times the mean squared endpoint-coordinate
error, plus 0.5 times a local distance loss and 0.1 times a local angle loss.
For reference distance $d_{ij}$ and radius sum $\ell_{ij}$, the local weight is
$w_{ij}=\operatorname{sigmoid}[(1.30-d_{ij}/\ell_{ij})/0.10]$ for distinct atoms.
The distance loss compares predicted and reference distances after division by
$\ell_{ij}$. The angle loss compares the cosines of pairs of neighbor directions,
weighted by $w_{ij}w_{ik}$ for distinct $i,j,k$. Each loss is normalized by its
weight sum within each molecule and averaged over molecules and both passes.
These terms use reference coordinates, with no chemical-bond targets.

\paragraph{Reference selection for the geometric head.}
A fixed hash order selects 4,096 rows from the same 20,000 training structures.
Of these, 3,783 pass the declared graph, contact-connectivity, overlap,
closed-shell and geometry checks. Every selection outcome is retained. The
inferred graphs serve this reference screen; the geometric network receives
only coordinates, element identities and progress. Evaluation compositions
are excluded from the training references.

We sample $p$ uniformly from $[0.4,0.95]$ and add centered Gaussian noise with
per-coordinate scale $s(p)=0.06+0.30(1-p)$\,\AA. Fifteen percent of examples
instead use the unperturbed reference and a zero target. For the other examples,
the target is $(X_{\rm ref}-\widetilde H)/(1-p)$. A common molecular scale caps
the largest per-atom target norm at $4p^2$\,\AA\ per unit progress. This preserves
zero total target velocity. Squared vector errors receive weight 0.25 on
hydrogens and one on heavy atoms, normalized by the sum of weights within each
molecule. The network uses AdamW at learning rate $10^{-3}$ for 10,000 updates
of batch 32, gradient-norm limit one, and EMA decay capped at 0.995. The final
EMA is used without checkpoint selection.

\paragraph{Geometric-field architecture.}
The head has 7,747 parameters: a 16-dimensional element embedding, a learned
linear neighbor-embedding update, and two width-64 hidden layers with SiLU
activations. A smooth contact weight is obtained from the current endpoint
distance as $\operatorname{sigmoid}[(1.30-d_{ij}/\ell_{ij})/0.20]$.
Node features combine the atom embedding with its contact-weighted neighboring
element embeddings. Pair features are symmetric sums and products of these
node features, distances, coordination summaries, six radial basis functions,
the radius sum, and $p,p^2$.

Three bounded coefficients multiply the normalized relative vector and two
bounded radial rescalings of it. Each pair contributes opposite vectors to its
two atoms. Dividing by three times the molecule's maximum weighted degree
(floored at one), and multiplying by $4p^2$, bounds every atom's correction
norm by $4p^2$. The final layer is initialized to zero. Rotation, reflection,
permutation and centering checks exercise the complete module. A same-size
variant supplied directional-moment features and nonradial vector directions;
it did not improve over the radial version in the development study.

\paragraph{Combining geometric and physical feedback.}
The geometric head acts first on the provisional endpoint. The physical head
then uses that updated endpoint, together with the current trajectory state,
to predict its correction. The geometric strength is one and physical strength
is four. Both small networks and the backbone remain fixed during generation.
There are 128 backbone, 64 geometry-head and 64 physical-head calls per main-model
output. The 7,106-parameter physical head is reused from the initial FM fit;
geometric refinement adds no eSEN queries. Geometry-head fitting uses no backbone
forward evaluations. The four thousand backbone updates contribute 256,000
additional backbone training-example forwards per fit, and geometric-head
fitting contributes 320,000 small-network training-example forwards.

\paragraph{Development and fixed-benchmark evaluation.}
The recovery and geometric-field variants were compared on 24 training-disjoint
validation compositions, 12 in each of the 17--28 and 29--40 atom strata.
Each of two fitted models generated 16 samples per composition. Table~\ref{tab:geometry-development}
reports the complete selected development sequence. The radial geometric field
and recovery with local geometry supervision were selected for combination.
All choices were then fixed before new outputs were generated on the existing
64-composition benchmark. That benchmark had been used in earlier project work.

\paragraph{Three additional fits.}
We repeated the selected continuation and geometric-field recipe for three
additional original FM parents, retaining each paired physical head. Their
geometry-and-force successes were 61, 101 and 121 out of 1,024, versus 22, 54
and 51 for the respective initial force-corrected generators. The paired gains
are 3.81, 4.59 and 6.84 points, averaging 5.08 points, with crossed 95\% interval
$[3.09,7.52]$. Across all five fits, 493 of 5,120 outputs pass geometry and force,
compared with 227 for the initial model. Geometry alone passes 494 outputs,
with distinct inferred connectivities within each composition and fit.
