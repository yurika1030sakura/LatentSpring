Physical correction improves FM joint yield by 7.03 points (95\% interval $[2.73,11.91]$), with gains of 6.25 and 7.81 in the two fits. GAGA gains 6.64 points ($[3.71,9.77]$), also improving in both fits. Thus the method benefits both velocity and noise predictors on the independently trained EGNN.

After matched physical supervision, FM minus GAGA is $-2.54$ points ($[-6.64,1.76]$), and the difference between their within-parent gains is $+0.39$ ($[-4.30,5.47]$). Neither comparison resolves an ordering between the generators. Intervals resample compositions conditional on the two fitted parents and heads, with strengths selected before test generation.
