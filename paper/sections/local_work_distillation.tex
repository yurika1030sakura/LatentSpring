\section{Local force-escorted work distillation}\label{app:local-work}
This separate experiment brings energy/force feedback into training while keeping
the positive source-comparison checkpoints frozen. Energy-weighted flow matching
and weighted physical-sample training already exist
\citep{dern2025energyweighted,fab}; escorted work identities are also established
\citep{escorted2008}. Our local construction is an application of these principles,
not a new global Boltzmann-sampling theorem.

\paragraph{A specified local reference and target.}
For a generated FIT anchor $A$ with accepted perceived graph $G_A$, define
$q_A(X)=\mathcal N_{\HH_N}(A,\sigma_A^2I)$.
At $kT=0.0258519998$ eV (300 K), remove net force and set
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_A=\min\left(0.03\,\text{\AA},
 \sqrt{\frac{0.10\,\text{\AA}\,kT}{\|F_+(A)\|}}\right),\qquad
 \delta_A=\frac{\sigma_A^2}{kT}F_+(A).
\end{equation}
A vanishing force selects the maximal width. These quantities are fixed before
drawing $X\sim q_A$. The escort $Y=X+\delta_A$ is a translation on the centered
subspace and has determinant one. The local unnormalized target is
\begin{equation}
 \rho_A(Y)=q_A(Y)\exp\!\left[-\frac{E_+(Y)-E_+(A)}{kT}\right]
 \mathbf1_{G_A}(Y),
\end{equation}
where the support requires the same labelled perceived bond orders, formal charges
and radical counts, as well as the existing connected/nonoverlap assay. These are
derived structural checks, not supplied bond labels or a bond-classification loss.
This target includes a Gaussian restraint around $A$ and does not equal the
unrestricted molecular Boltzmann law.

\paragraph{Complete local work.}
With reference Hamiltonian $H_0=-kT\log q_A$ and target Hamiltonian
$H_1=H_0+E_+-E_+(A)$ plus hard support, the generalized quench work is
\begin{equation}
 W_A(X)=E_+(Y)-E_+(A)+kT[\log q_A(X)-\log q_A(Y)]
\end{equation}
on valid support, and $+\infty$ otherwise. Change of variables gives
$\mathbb E_{q_A}[\exp(-W_A/kT)]=\int\rho_A(Y)\,dY$.
The two reference-density terms correct for the escort's proposal shift.
Energy difference alone would omit them. Two analytic tests verify constant work
for a constant-force escort and the normalizer/weighted mean for a quadratic
energy. These tests validate the local arithmetic, not a global molecular sampler.

\paragraph{Training and controls.}
Each continuation selects eight distinct 17--28-atom TRAIN compositions by a fixed
metadata hash and generates 32 new FIT anchors per composition. They are excluded
from the evaluated compositions. Invalid anchors are recorded rather than
replenished. Each valid anchor produces eight escorted particles. Particles outside
its declared support receive zero weight. The work arm samples training endpoints
using self-normalized $\exp(-W_A/kT)$ weights; the unweighted escort arm samples the
same valid particles uniformly. A replay arm uses the retained raw anchors, and a
frozen model supplies the reference. All-zero-particle anchors are recorded and
excluded identically from all student pools.

Each student alternates 500 original-reference FM updates with 500 generated-FIT
endpoint updates, sharing initialization, anchor draws, optimization and sampling
budgets. No oracle is queried by the final generator. Physics enters through
training targets. The generator retains its legacy constant temperature input;
this is not a model trained to predict different temperatures. Finite-particle
normalization biases the teacher relative to the exact normalized local target.
Its ESS refers only to eight local particles, not global generator outputs.
Original-reference mixing and unknown chemical-isomer weights further prevent a
global equilibrium claim, even if the local identity is exact.
