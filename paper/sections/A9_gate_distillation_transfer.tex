\section{Matched gate pretraining and larger-cohort check}
\label{app:gate-distillation-transfer}

To test whether sparse utility feedback limits the source-force model, we
compare direct utility optimization for 800 steps with 500 steps of normalized
oracle log-gate target fitting followed by 300 utility steps. For finite true
MH ratio $R$ and floor $e^{-B}$, the labels are
\begin{equation}
 \ell_f^*=\operatorname{clip}(R,-B,0),\qquad
 \ell_r^*=\operatorname{clip}(-R,-B,0).
\end{equation}
They retain ordinary MH acceptance and are pointwise minimal under the floor
subject to that retained probability. This elementary identity supplies training
labels, not a deployable cheap oracle: constructing them requires the proposed
energy after it has been queried. Positive log-gate underprediction relative to
these labels bounds the corresponding loss of accepted probability; empirical
label error does not certify a uniform unseen-state bound or mixing rate.

Both recipes use the same source-force architecture, data, seeds, index draws
and total step count. Adam resets at step 500 for both. Exact index corrections
retain each declared empirical objective and all failures. No validation is used
at the boundary checkpoint. The linear and neural versions each have two seeds,
for eight frozen arms. All final metrics, fixed/thinning controls and 12,776
supported-pair cases pass independent checks, including both gate orientations.
All eight sampled-index streams are reconstructed and matched across recipes;
24 phase-boundary/final head derivatives pass the declared finite-difference
tolerances. No new physical queries are used.

Pretrained-minus-direct neural differences are $-4.30\times10^{-5}$ and
$-6.81\times10^{-5}$\,eV per expected call, with descriptive 95\% parent intervals
$[-6.58,-2.33]\times10^{-5}$ and $[-11.17,-3.15]\times10^{-5}$.
Both pretrained neural models also lose to fixed physical screening on this
cohort. Increasing matched training time and changing initialization supervision
has not resolved the competitive deficit.

\paragraph{Larger reused internal cohort.}
Because the fixed physical screen's ranking reverses between fitting and internal
selection parents, we freeze an evaluation-only check on 48 other parents and
six compositions. Two composition identities were absent from gate fitting.
These physical trajectories were previously used to evaluate scalar models;
they are not an untouched final test. Neither these parents nor the original
12 selection parents enter the new models' fitting. We retain each physical
trajectory's first 128-call prefix, including the corrected condition-5 control,
for 96 prefixes, 1,620 joint attempts and 1,582 scored pairs. All original energy,
force, electronic-state and numerical-repair provenance is retained. No models
are updated and no new oracle calls are used. Six independent audits verify
30,058 model/pair cases with maximum discrepancy $2.79\times10^{-13}$.

Six compositions are weighted equally despite their unequal parent counts;
uncertainty resamples parents within composition and retains both trajectories.
The all-composition no-screen rate is $0.0090515$\,eV per expected call, versus
$0.0086175$ for fixed physical screening. Pretrained neural rates are
$0.0088513$ and $0.0087255$: point improvements of 2.71\% and 1.25\% over physical
screening, but decreases of 2.21\% and 3.60\% against no screening. Both sets of
intervals span zero. No tested screen establishes a rate improvement over no
screening on this larger cohort, and neural-versus-linear intervals also span
zero. The two unseen compositions do not establish a consistent neural benefit.
These results stop scale-up of the tested screen recipes. They remain
fixed-source prefix expectations, not measurements of new screened chains,
actual savings or equilibrium accuracy. Conclusions require the no-screen comparison as well as
comparisons between screens. All intervals are descriptive and not
multiplicity-adjusted.
