LatentSpring: Harmonic Sources and Physical Corrections for Molecular Generation

Generating a molecule from atomic composition leaves both its chemical connectivity and three-dimensional geometry to be determined. We present LatentSpring, which combines a harmonic-mixture source with learned geometric and physical endpoint corrections. Random auxiliary trees and atomic length scales organize the starting coordinates without known bonds. Recovery training teaches the flow to correct geometric perturbations, and two small networks refine its provisional endpoint using reference geometry and force feedback. On 64 unseen compositions, five fitted models achieve 35.35% graph-valid yield with force RMS at most 5 eV/Å, compared with 8.75% for GAGA and 8.15% for EDM. Requiring local geometry checks as well gives a 9.63% yield, up from 4.43% for the initial force-corrected flow. Three additional training repetitions reproduce this geometric improvement. A matched continuation control separates the benefit of geometric recovery from extra training. Both corrections act in coordinate space and connect to flow velocities or diffusion noise predictions through the same endpoint displacement. Generation uses neural evaluations, with no energy calls or geometry optimization.
