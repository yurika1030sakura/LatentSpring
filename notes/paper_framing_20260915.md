# User-directed publication framing

The user correctly distinguishes the paper from a complete experimental diary.
Focus the submission on supported contributions. Discarded architecture attempts
and capped relaxation are retained in research records but removed from the
manuscript when no claims depend on them. Preserve the actual raw graph rate,
approximate-potential scope, data/continuation conditions and material controls.
Do not claim rapid relaxation, global Boltzmann output or a work-specific neural
advantage without evidence. Keep Jarzynski as a qualified local-work construction
and mechanism study, as requested by the user.

Primary-paper organization checked:

- Flow Matching for Generative Modeling (ICLR2023): foregrounds the training/path
  construction and targeted generation results. Original paper:
  https://openreview.net/pdf?id=PqvMRDCJT9t (search index returned the conference
  paper; direct OpenReview fetch encountered its browser challenge).
- GeoLDM (ICML2023): motivates a geometric latent representation, tests molecular
  generation/conditioning, and isolates equivariant latent features with focused
  ablations. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/xu23n/xu23n.pdf
- ET-Flow (NeurIPS2024): emphasizes precision, source/path choices and efficiency;
  key source/alignment ablations and larger-molecule limits remain documented.
  https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/e8bd617e7dd0394ceadf37b4a7773179-Paper-Conference.pdf
- Transferable Boltzmann Generators (NeurIPS2024): centers transfer across molecular
  systems and relevant distribution/weighting evidence; it explicitly relies on
  existing theorems. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/5035a409f5798e188079e236f437e522-Paper-Conference.pdf

The writing lesson is a clear problem, specific construction, directly supporting
comparisons and concise limitations. These papers do not provide evidence that
hiding material outcomes is necessary, nor that matching a reported percentage
improvement guarantees acceptance. Our positive framing is latent spatial source
structure plus measured training-only physical feedback. No new physical law or
success in every possible setting is a prerequisite for a scoped submission.
