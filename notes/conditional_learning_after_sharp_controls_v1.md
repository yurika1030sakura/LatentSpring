The existing 32-context diagnostic identifies the next learning issue. A tangent
force at one direction does not identify a vMF concentration for an otherwise
fixed context. Multi-direction probes identify a local surrogate. However, the
student's withheld-parent KL is dominated by direction error, not width error.
Useful learning therefore requires both conditional identification and calibrated
direction prediction across contexts. A larger concentration bound alone failed.

## Next bounded implementation

This is an implementation plan, not a frozen molecular experiment or a validated
new method. Freeze the exact protocol, source/checkpoint hashes, seeds and split
before new queries; test and independently replay every new probe construction.

1. Build fixed-context angular labels on the new TRAINING parents. A bounded
   design is one warm-context root per parent plus two independently drawn
   physical exchange proposals per parent, with both moved roots probed when the
   proposal is structurally supported. Use single draws without support-conditioned
   resampling. Preserve all rejected proposals and every rank/approximation failure.
   Fit four signed off-axis probes and retain two diagonal checks per context,
   reusing the audited warm force and querying each supported exchanged center
   once. The maximum additional cost is 6,144 paired raw calls for all 96 parents.
   Both passives and radius stay fixed within each conditional probe set.

2. Split parent/composition groups for internal selection before fitting. The two
   already evaluated development panels and reserved outcomes cannot select
   weights. Internal held-out training compositions remain development checks,
   not a blind final benchmark. Include the original audited 32 training contexts
   only with their provenance and 10,108-call source/probe cost kept explicit.

3. On the same data and parameterization, compare direct angular-force fitting,
   normalized teacher-KL fitting, and a small, prespecified physical-prior
   regularization ablation. Initialize a meaningful physical proposal and test
   direction error, proper density loss and withheld finite work. A physical-prior
   KL penalty is established regularization, not a novelty claim. Do not launch
   molecular sampling for another student that only improves training fit.

4. If a candidate passes these internal learnability checks, freeze its molecular
   test against the strong physical controls and representative learned baselines.
   The existing physical paths reach 1,024 calls per parent. Even the conservative
   training-cost ceiling 10,108 + 11,910 + 6,144 = 28,162 calls allows an exactly
   cost-matched comparison to a 512-call learned trajectory on either the 72- or
   96-parent cohort using these already audited physical paths. Use the actual
   completed training cost, not this ceiling, in the final allocation. Preserve
   all source attempts, unsuccessful compositions and correlations. New learned
   sampling remains necessary; cached physics does not constitute a learner win.

The proposed contribution must be a useful conditional-learning intervention for
joint molecular connectivity/geometry moves, with defensible evidence. vMF
identities, force fitting, regularization and exact MH are not new physics or
independent AI novelty. If this route still fails, use the audited fragment maps
to investigate a broader proposal rather than recycling the rejected singleton
claim. The ICLR objective remains active and scientifically unachieved.
